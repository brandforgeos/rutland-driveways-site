Outdoor kitchens in Uppingham

Outdoor kitchens in Uppingham

Uppingham is a hilltop town, and few gardens here are truly flat. A worktop that falls an inch over its length is annoying to cook on and sends spills the wrong way, so the work starts with getting a level platform on ground that wants to run downhill. From there we build block or stone-faced counters, fit weatherproof worktops and plan where the grill, oven, sink and fridge go so that each has the clearance and services it needs.

Treat the figures on this page as guide prices. Once we have walked the garden and measured the falls, checked the distance back to the house for gas, power and water, and seen how materials will get in where access to the rear is tight, you get a fixed price in writing. We work across Uppingham and the surrounding villages, including Lyddington, Seaton, Bisbrooke, Glaston, Wing, Caldecott and Morcott, and we also cover Oakham, Market Harborough and Corby.

Uppingham specifically

What makes Uppingham different

The town sits on Northampton Sand ironstone, which usually drains better than the Vale clay at Oakham but changes within a single street. The bigger factor for an outdoor kitchen is gradient. Water from the slope above will run towards a new base unless it is intercepted, and a level kitchen on a falling garden may need a retaining edge or a raised platform. Many centre properties have courtyards rather than gardens, so compact layouts with a single run of counters are common.

Behind a house near the High Street or the Market Place, a typical kitchen is a short straight run in a walled courtyard: grill, a slim worktop and a fridge, with careful thought about smoke near neighbouring windows. On the Victorian and interwar roads out of town, or the later estates to the north and east, there is usually room for an L-shape with a sink and a pizza oven.

Outdoor kitchens in Uppingham: questions

How much is an outdoor kitchen in Uppingham?
An Uppingham kitchen with built-in cabinets, worktop and appliance space starts from about £6,000, and is typically £8,000–£20,000 once a grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge are included. Gas, power and water connections are priced on top, depending on the distance to the house. Any stepped or raised base on a sloping plot is included in the fixed written price.
Does an outdoor kitchen need planning in Uppingham or nearby villages?
A kitchen at the back of the house does not normally need planning, though cover over it must stay within the outbuilding height and size limits. Uppingham's centre is a conservation area without an Article 4 direction. Lyddington, Seaton, Ayston, Preston, Ridlington and Belton-in-Rutland do have Article 4 directions, aimed at front walls, gates and parking spaces.
Can you fit an outdoor kitchen into a small courtyard?
Yes, a single run of counters with a grill and a fridge fits many Uppingham courtyards. We look closely at where smoke will drift, the clearance a grill needs and how materials get in, because centre properties often have narrow or no side access.
My garden slopes. Where is the best place for the kitchen?
Usually on the flattest area close to the house, which keeps service runs short and the base simpler. If the only good spot is on the slope, we build a level platform and deal with the water running down towards it.
