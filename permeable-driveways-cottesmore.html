Permeable driveways in Cottesmore

Permeable driveways in Cottesmore

Family homes in Cottesmore often want a wider drive, and the usual worry is that more hard surface means more water on the pavement. A permeable driveway answers that, because rain soaks through the surface and an open-graded base rather than running off. We build them in resin bound, permeable block paving and stabilised gravel. In a large village with plenty of later housing alongside its ironstone and limestone cottages, the right finish depends on the house in front of it.

The ground is the bigger variable. Cottesmore sits on mixed ground at the edge of the limestone, and the North Brook rises nearby, so drainage changes from plot to plot. We dig a test hole before deciding between a soakaway and a permeable build-up, and where clay will not take water, we line the base and drain it to a controlled outlet. The fixed written price reflects what the test shows.

Cottesmore specifically

What makes Cottesmore different

Cottesmore has a conservation area covering the historic village and no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings. The old centre is ironstone and limestone cottages, with a good deal of later housing around it, and Kendrew Barracks, the former RAF Cottesmore, sits just outside the village. Rutland's flood strategy traces the North Brook through Cottesmore towards Empingham and Ryhall, so homes near its course need the water route thought through carefully.

A typical Cottesmore job is widening the drive of a later family house so another car fits, taking up the old surface and part of the lawn. We dig a test hole, then build an open-graded base sized for that plot's ground, lining it if the clay will not drain. Resin bound and permeable block both suit this kind of frontage.

Permeable driveways in Cottesmore: questions

How much does a permeable drive cost in Cottesmore?
Permeable block paving is £140–£210 per m², permeable resin bound £150–£220 per m² and stabilised gravel £60–£100 per m², all in, as guides. If the test hole shows clay, a lined system with a controlled outlet adds to that. A widened drive is priced on its full area, and the fixed figure comes in writing after the visit.
Can I widen my drive onto the lawn without planning permission?
Cottesmore has no Article 4 direction, so outside listed buildings the normal rules apply. A new or replacement front driveway needs no planning permission if it is permeable, or if its water drains to a lawn or border on your own land, so building the widened area as a permeable system is the straightforward route.
Why can my neighbour's drive drain differently from mine?
Cottesmore sits on mixed ground at the edge of the limestone, so one plot may drain freely while the next sits on clay. That is why we dig a test hole at your property rather than relying on what worked next door. The result decides whether the base soaks away or is lined with a controlled outlet.
Does the North Brook affect drives in Cottesmore?
The North Brook rises near Cottesmore, and Rutland's flood strategy traces its course through the village. For homes near it, we take extra care over where water from the drive goes, and a lined permeable base with a controlled outlet is sometimes a better answer than relying on soakage.
