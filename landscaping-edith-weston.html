Landscaping in Edith Weston

Landscaping in Edith Weston: what is involved

A garden transformation takes a garden from an idea to a finished space under one contract: levels, drainage, paving, walls, steps, beds, lawn, fencing and lighting, built in the right order.

Edith Weston sits on the south shore of Rutland Water, a stone village with a green and a number of substantial houses, next to St George's Barracks. Terraces, outdoor rooms and lighting designed around the views are the typical projects here.

We cover Edith Weston (LE15) and the villages around it, including North Luffenham, South Luffenham, Lyndon, Manton and Normanton.

Edith Weston specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Edith Weston

Edith Weston is on the limestone plateau above the reservoir, and most plots drain reasonably well, though clay pockets turn up. We test the ground before designing the base.

Edith Weston has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings, with natural materials the right choice in the conservation area.

Rutland Water itself carries negligible flood risk according to Rutland's flood strategy, and Edith Weston is not one of the places it names for river flooding. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Rutland conservation areas · Rutland flood risk strategy.
