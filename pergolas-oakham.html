Louvred pergolas in Oakham

Louvred pergolas in Oakham

A louvred pergola gives an Oakham garden a covered room you can open to the sun or close against a shower. The frame is powder-coated aluminium and stands on its own posts, so nothing is fixed to the house wall. Tilt the louvres open for light and air; close them and the roof sheds rain into gutters hidden inside the frame, then down through the posts. Warm-white LED lighting is built in, and the sides can stay open or take privacy louvres, glass walls with sliding doors, or blinds.

Oakham asks more of the ground underneath than most towns we cover. Much of it sits on heavy clay that holds water all winter, and Rutland County Council's flood strategy lists the town as a surface water flood risk area of national significance. So before we price anything we look at the patio or lawn the pergola will stand on, dig where we need to, and decide whether the posts can fix to what is there or need concrete pads or a new patio.

Oakham specifically

What makes Oakham different

Clay is the main thing. A patio laid on a thin base over Vale of Catmose clay can move, and an aluminium frame fixed to it will show that movement in its louvres. We check the existing slabs are sound and properly bedded before fixing to them, and where they are not we put the posts on concrete pads. The water that runs down the posts also has to go somewhere, and in a town where water with nowhere to go is the commonest problem we fix, we design a soakaway, channel or connection on the visit.

Many Oakham enquiries come from the Barleythorpe and Oakham Heights estates, where a pergola goes over an existing rear patio, often a 3m × 4m frame against the back of the house with one side closed for privacy from the neighbours. On older semis off Brooke Road or Burley Road, gardens are longer and the pergola often sits part-way down, which usually means new pads or a new patio.

Louvred pergolas in Oakham: questions

How much does a louvred pergola cost in Oakham?
Prices start from £4,500 supplied and fitted for a 3m × 4m frame on a suitable existing base. On Oakham clay the base is often the variable, so concrete pads or a new patio, plus bigger sizes, motorised louvres or side screens, are priced after the site visit. You get a fixed price in writing once we have seen the garden.
Do I need planning permission for a pergola in Oakham?
A freestanding rear-garden pergola is normally permitted development if it stays behind the front of the house, is no more than 2.5m at the eaves, 2.5m overall within 2m of a boundary, not on a platform over 30cm, and keeps outbuildings under half the garden. In the Oakham conservation area it cannot sit between the side of the house and the boundary. Listed houses need permission from Rutland County Council.
Where does the rain from the roof go on clay ground?
Closed louvres send rain into gutters inside the frame and down through the posts. On Oakham's clay a bare outlet onto the patio just moves the puddle, so we plan the route on the visit: a channel, a soakaway sized after a test hole, or a connection into existing drainage where that is suitable.
Can a pergola go on my new-build patio in Barleythorpe?
Often, yes, but builder-grade patios are not always laid on a deep enough base for fixings. We lift the edge of a slab or check the bedding where the posts will land. If it is sound we fix straight to it; if not, we set the posts on concrete pads so the frame stays square.
