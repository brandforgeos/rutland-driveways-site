Louvred pergolas in Kettering

Louvred pergolas in Kettering

Kettering's gardens come in two broad shapes. Close to the centre and along the Headlands, Victorian and Edwardian terraces and villas tend to have long, narrow plots where a pergola has to fit between two boundaries. Out at Hanwood Park, planned for up to 5,500 homes, and in Barton Seagrave and the post-war estates, gardens are wider but the ground has often been compacted by the build, so what lies under the turf matters as much as the frame.

A louvred pergola works in both. It is a freestanding aluminium frame that does not need the house wall, with a roof of louvres that open for sun or close to shed rain into internal gutters and down the posts. In a narrow terrace garden, a frame within 2m of a boundary must stay at 2.5m overall to remain permitted development, and closing one side with privacy louvres or blinds can make the space far more usable.

Kettering specifically

What makes Kettering different

We do not assume anything about Kettering ground. It ranges from ironstone to clay, and new-build plots are often compacted subsoil under a thin layer of topsoil, which is poor for a soakaway and needs proper pads if there is no patio. Kettering has 26 conservation areas and several site-specific Article 4 directions, so we check your address on the visit; in any conservation area, a pergola cannot go between the side of the house and the boundary.

A typical Kettering terrace job is a 3m × 3m or 3m × 4m pergola at the back of a narrow garden, kept at 2.5m because it sits near both boundaries, with one closed side. At Hanwood Park it is more often a larger frame across the width of a new patio.

Sources: North Northamptonshire Article 4 directions · Hanwood Park.

Questions

Louvred pergolas in Kettering: questions

What will a pergola cost in Kettering?
Our Kettering guide price starts from £4,500 supplied and fitted, which buys a 3m × 4m louvred frame on a suitable existing base. Pads or a new patio, bigger frames, motorised louvres, glass walls and blinds are priced after the site visit, and the fixed price follows in writing.
Is my Kettering house affected by conservation area or Article 4 rules?
Possibly. Kettering has 26 conservation areas, including the town centre and Barton Seagrave, and its Article 4 directions cover individual streets or buildings. In a conservation area a pergola cannot sit beside the house, and a listed house needs permission from North Northamptonshire Council. We check your address before designing.
Can a pergola fit a narrow terrace garden?
Usually. Frames start at 3m × 3m. Because a narrow garden puts the pergola within 2m of a boundary, we keep it to 2.5m overall to stay within permitted development. Access for the frame through the house or a side passage is checked on the visit.
Why does new-build ground at Hanwood Park need checking?
New-build plots are often compacted subsoil under thin topsoil. That ground drains poorly, so the roof water from the posts needs a planned route, and if there is no sound patio the posts need concrete pads. We dig where the posts will stand before we price.
