Ground and conservation in Market Harborough

Market Harborough is on Jurassic mudstones and clays, which drain slowly, and the town sits where the River Welland meets the River Jordan. Water management decides most designs here, so we test the ground before choosing a soakaway or a permeable build-up. Clay around Market Harborough drains slowly, so a plain soakaway often fills and stays full. We run a percolation test first; where the ground will not take water, we use a lined system or crates with a controlled outlet.

The Market Harborough conservation area covers the historic town core and was designated in 1969, with separate conservation areas at Great Bowden and Lubenham. An Article 4 direction served in 2020 on land at Queen's Street, Little Bowden, covers gates, fences and walls.

The town centre flooded from surface water in September 2024, when a high River Welland stopped the drains discharging, and more than 50 properties flooded in July 2013. Permeable surfaces and attenuation are worth taking seriously here. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Driveway drainage in Market Harborough: questions

How much does a driveway drainage cost in Market Harborough?
A channel drain across a typical driveway or garage entrance costs about £1,000–£1,800 installed, or £120–£200 per metre for a heavy-duty, vehicle-rated channel. Soakaways are sized and priced after a percolation test. Your fixed price comes in writing after the site visit.
Do I need planning permission for a driveway drainage in Market Harborough?

Does the ground in Market Harborough make a difference?
Clay around Market Harborough drains slowly, so a plain soakaway often fills and stays full. We run a percolation test first; where the ground will not take water, we use a lined system or crates with a controlled outlet.
Why does my drive puddle?
Usually the falls are wrong, the base has sunk in places, or the water has nowhere to go at the bottom. We check which before suggesting a channel drain.
Can drive water go into the drains?
Not into the foul sewer. It has to go to a soakaway, a permeable area or, with approval, a surface water sewer.
