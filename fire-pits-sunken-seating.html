Fire pits & sunken seating

What it is

We build fire pits set into the patio and sunken seating areas dug into the garden, with retaining walls, built-in benches, steps and drainage at the bottom so the area does not fill with water.

Fire pits & sunken seating

The part people forget

A sunken seating area is a hole in the garden, and holes fill with water. The retaining walls need footings and drainage behind them, and the floor needs a drain or soakaway at its lowest point. That is most of the job, and it is the part we are best at.

Fire pits & sunken seating

Safe fires

We line the fire pit, keep it a safe distance from fences, trees and buildings, and use heat-resistant materials around it.

Fire pits & sunken seating

Planning

A fire pit or sunken seating area in a rear garden does not normally need planning permission. Keep fires a safe distance from fences, trees and buildings, and be considerate of neighbours: smoke can be a statutory nuisance. Planning Portal: outbuildings

Questions

Things people ask us

How much does a fire pit cost?
A built-in stone or brick fire pit typically costs £1,200–£2,000, and a fire pit with built-in seating from about £3,500. A full sunken seating area with retaining walls and drainage is priced per job, usually as part of a wider garden build.
Will a sunken seating area fill with water?
Not if it is drained. We put a drain or soakaway at the low point, sized for the ground, before the paving goes down.
Gas or wood fire pit?
Wood gives the real fire and the smell; gas is cleaner and turns on at a switch. A gas pit needs a Gas Safe engineer.
