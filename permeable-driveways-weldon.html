Permeable driveways in Weldon

Permeable driveways in Weldon

Weldon is famous for Weldon stone, the limestone quarried here for centuries, and the village still sits over the limestone and ironstone that were worked. For a permeable driveway, that history is the first thing to understand. Natural rock may let water through an open-graded base readily, while clay or fill in the same garden may not. We dig to find out, then design the base and drainage for your plot rather than for the village in general.

Rutland Driveways & Landscaping builds permeable drives in Weldon in resin bound, permeable block paving and stabilised gravel. Where the ground will take water, the drive soaks away through its own base. Where it will not, we line the system and drain it to a controlled outlet. The Willow Brook runs past the village, so drainage needs designing rather than assuming. The fixed written price follows the visit, once the dig has shown what we are building on.

Weldon specifically

What makes Weldon different

Weldon's historic centre of stone houses is surrounded by later estates that now join it to Corby, so frontages range from cottage plots in the conservation area to modern estate drives. The conservation area covers the historic village and was appraised in 2009, and North Northamptonshire has no Article 4 direction in Weldon affecting driveways. The Environment Agency operates the Weldon flood storage reservoir on the Willow Brook. North Northamptonshire Council handles planning, highways and building control.

A typical Weldon job is a later estate house on the Corby side, where an old drive is lifted and the dig reveals whether the plot is on natural rock, clay or fill. We build the open-graded base to suit and finish in permeable block or resin bound. In the stone-built centre, a natural-toned finish usually sits better against Weldon stone.

You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Questions

Permeable driveways in Weldon: questions

How much does a permeable driveway cost in Weldon?
Guide figures are £140–£210 per m² for permeable block paving, £150–£220 per m² for a permeable resin bound driveway and £60–£100 per m² for stabilised gravel, all in. If the dig shows clay or fill that will not drain, a lined system adds to that. We send a fixed price in writing once we know.
Is there an Article 4 direction in Weldon?
There is not. North Northamptonshire has no Article 4 direction in Weldon affecting driveways. A permeable new or replacement front drive, or one that drains to a lawn or border on your own land, needs no planning permission, though homes in the conservation area and listed buildings still deserve a check first.
Why do you dig before pricing in Weldon?
Because the ground can change from natural limestone or ironstone to clay or fill within a single garden. A permeable base that suits rock may not drain on clay. The dig tells us which we are dealing with, and whether the base can soak away or needs lining with a controlled outlet.
Does the Willow Brook affect drives in Weldon?
For homes close to it, it can. The Willow Brook runs past Weldon, where the Environment Agency operates a flood storage reservoir. We take more care over where drive water goes on those plots, and a lined permeable base with a controlled outlet is sometimes the sensible design.
