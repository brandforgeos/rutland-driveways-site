Bin stores in Weldon

Bin stores in Weldon: what is involved

We fit composite and aluminium bin stores for one, two or three wheelie bins, on a level paved pad with a path to the kerb, so the bins are hidden and easy to get out on collection day.

Weldon is an old stone village on the east side of Corby, famous for Weldon stone, the limestone quarried here for centuries. Its historic centre of stone houses is surrounded by later estates that now join it to Corby.

We cover Weldon (NN17) and the villages around it, including Great Oakley, Little Oakley, Deene, Brigstock and Stanion.

Weldon specifically

Ground and conservation in Weldon

Weldon sits over the limestone and ironstone that were quarried here, and ground can change from natural rock to clay or fill within a garden. The Willow Brook runs past the village, so drainage needs designing rather than assuming. On the clay around Weldon we dig the base below the topsoil and build it thicker, so it does not move with the seasons.

Weldon has a conservation area covering the historic village, appraised in 2009. North Northamptonshire has no Article 4 direction in Weldon affecting driveways, porches or extensions.

Sources: Weldon conservation area (Northamptonshire HER) · North Northamptonshire conservation areas.

Bin stores in Weldon: questions

How much does a bin store cost in Weldon?
A single wheelie bin store fitted on a level paved base starts from about £1,000. Two and three bin stores, and any new path to the kerb, are priced on the visit. Your fixed price comes in writing after the site visit.
Do I need planning permission for a bin store in Weldon?
A bin store at the side or back of the house is normally permitted development. In front of the house, a free-standing store technically falls outside householder permitted development, so we check with the council before building one there. Listed buildings and the Weldon conservation area have tighter rules, so we check your address before anything is designed.
Does the ground in Weldon make a difference?
On the clay around Weldon we dig the base below the topsoil and build it thicker, so it does not move with the seasons.
Will my bins fit?
We measure your bins first. Standard 240-litre wheelie bins fit, and we check the lid can open fully inside the store.
Can a bin store go at the front?
Often, but in front of the house it technically needs the council's say-so, so we check before building it.
