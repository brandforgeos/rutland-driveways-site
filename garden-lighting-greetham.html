Garden lighting in Greetham

Garden lighting in Greetham

Many Greetham homes are widening their drives for more cars or replacing them after decades of gravel. If lighting is on your wish list, that is the moment to plan it. A duct laid along the edge of a new drive or path costs very little compared with cutting one in later, and it means low lights can mark the route from the road to the front door and round to the garden. We design low-voltage schemes with path, step and riser lights, uplighters and soft light around seating. Prices here are guides.

The historic core is stone cottages and farm buildings, and stone takes light well: a barn wall or a gable lit softly from the ground gives a garden real character at night. Later infill and estate housing tends to have more straightforward gardens, where a lit patio and a couple of lit borders are the usual brief. Greetham Valley sits on the edge of the village. Wherever you are, we keep fittings pointing down and away from neighbouring windows.

Greetham specifically

What makes Greetham different

Greetham sits on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which generally drains well, so buried uplighters and in-ground path lights usually work here without fuss. The exception is low-lying ground: Rutland's flood strategy names Greetham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, so on those plots we raise the transformer and connections and use wall-mounted fittings. Greetham has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, and stone-coloured or dark fittings suit the village best.

A typical Greetham job pairs lighting with other work. A house replacing an old gravel drive has ducts laid along the edge, with low lights leading to the front door and a step light at the porch. At the back, a lit patio and an uplighter on a stone wall or barn complete the scheme.

Questions

Garden lighting in Greetham: questions

How much is garden lighting in Greetham?
A path, step and border lighting scheme typically costs up to about £1,500 installed, and a full multi-zone garden scheme £2,000–£6,000 or more. A new outdoor circuit is priced separately. These are guide prices; you get a fixed price in writing after our visit.
Do I need planning permission for lighting in Greetham?
Garden lighting does not need planning permission, and Greetham has no Article 4 direction, though it has a conservation area. A new outdoor mains circuit is notifiable electrical work, done and certified by a registered electrician. Lights should point down and away from neighbours' windows.
We are replacing our gravel drive. Can lighting go in at the same time?
Yes, and it is the best time. Ducts laid along the edge of the new drive or path mean cables are protected and never sit under the surface. You can fit the lights straight away or leave the ducts ready for later.
Can you light an old stone barn or farm building in the garden?
Yes. A barn wall lit from the ground with one or two warm uplighters is one of the most effective things you can do in a stone village. Ground-mounted fittings avoid drilling into the old stone, and the light is aimed up the wall, not across to neighbours.
