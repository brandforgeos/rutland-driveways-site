What a fitted bin store costs

What is included in the price

From about £1,000 covers one composite or aluminium store, the level paved pad it stands on, and fitting it so the lid and doors open fully with the bin inside. We measure your bins first so the store suits the ones you actually have.

Larger stores follow the same approach on a wider pad. If the route to the kerb is gravel, grass or steps, a new level path can be added, priced once we have seen it.

What a fitted bin store costs

What makes it cost more or less

  • The number of bins and the width of pad that needs
  • Composite slats or aluminium, and the door style
  • What the pad replaces: lawn, gravel or broken paving
  • Whether a path to the kerb is needed, and how long it is
  • Slopes or steps between the store and the kerb
  • Planning checks for a store in front of the house
What a fitted bin store costs

Bin store costs around Rutland, Stamford and Corby

Bin stores usually sit at the side or front of the house, so local planning rules play a bigger part than they do for back-garden work. In Stamford's conservation area, the Article 4 direction removes permitted development for hard surfaces and boundaries facing a road, so a new paved pad at the front can need permission, and many older streets have tight frontages. Uppingham's centre has plenty of small frontages and rear courtyards too. On new estates such as Priors Hall Park in Corby and Hanwood Park in Kettering, gardens are often compacted subsoil, so the pad needs a properly dug base rather than sitting on the surface.

What a fitted bin store costs

What to check in any quote

  • That your actual bins have been measured and the lids open fully
  • The pad: level, paved and on a dug base
  • Whether a path to the kerb is included
  • Whether planning has been checked for a front-garden position
  • Door and hinge material, and how the store is fixed down

Questions

Things people ask us

What is the cheapest bin store option?
A single store on a level paved base, from about £1,000, is the simplest job. Placing it beside an existing hard path, so no new route to the kerb is needed, keeps the cost down further.
Is a fitted bin store worth paying for?
It tidies the front of the house, stops bins blowing over and keeps them off the drive. A store on a level pad with a clear route to the kerb also makes collection day easier for everyone in the house.
Does a composite bin store need maintenance?
Very little. Composite slats and aluminium doors do not need painting; a wash down now and then and a check that hinges and catches move freely is usually all it takes. Keep the pad clear of leaves so water runs off.
How is payment handled on a small job?
Payment terms are in the written quote with the fixed price, just as on larger work. Bin stores are often added to a driveway or path job, in which case they sit in the same quote.
How long is a bin store quote valid?
The written quote says how long its price holds. If you come back after that date, we check the store and paving prices again and confirm the figure in writing before booking, so there are no surprises on the day.
