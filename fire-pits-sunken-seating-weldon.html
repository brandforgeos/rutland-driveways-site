Fire pits & sunken seating in Weldon

Weldon stone, the limestone quarried here for centuries, gives the village its character, and a fire pit or sunken seating area in a Weldon garden looks best when it respects that. We build pits lined and set into the patio, and sunken seating with retaining walls on proper footings, built-in benches, steps and drainage at the bottom. Benches and walls can be faced in stone, brick or render to match the house. Prices are guides, and the fixed price comes in writing after the visit.

The historic centre has stone houses with established gardens, while the later estates that now join Weldon to Corby have regular rear plots with a lawn and patio. Both suit a fire pit with built-in seating, though the older gardens usually call for a more careful choice of facing material. We also cover Great Oakley, Little Oakley, Deene, Brigstock and Stanion, where the ground can be just as varied. Every garden starts with a dig.

Weldon specifically

What makes Weldon different

Weldon sits over the limestone and ironstone that were quarried here, and ground can change from natural rock to clay or fill within a single garden. That affects a sunken seating area in two ways: retaining walls need footings on firm ground, and the drain or soakaway at the bottom must suit what is actually there. The Willow Brook runs past the village, where the Environment Agency operates the Weldon flood storage reservoir, so drainage is designed rather than assumed.

A typical Weldon job is a house on one of the later estates, with a level lawn and a basic patio. We dig a sunken seating area beside the patio, find firm ground for the retaining wall footings, face the benches in brick or a stone to suit the village, and set a lined fire pit at the centre with a drain at the low point.

You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Fire pits & sunken seating in Weldon: questions

How much does a fire pit cost in Weldon?
Weldon guide prices: a built-in stone or brick fire pit typically costs £1,200–£2,000, and a fire pit with built-in seating from about £3,500. A Weldon sunken seating area with retaining walls and drainage is priced per job, usually as part of a wider garden build. Variable ground can affect footing depth, so we fix the price after digging.
Is Weldon's conservation area a problem for a fire pit?
Weldon has a conservation area covering the historic village, appraised in 2009, and North Northamptonshire has no Article 4 direction in Weldon affecting driveways, porches or extensions. Either way, a fire pit or sunken seating area in a Weldon rear garden does not normally need planning permission. Keep any fire clear of fences, trees and stone buildings.
Could our garden be on old quarry fill?
It is possible. Weldon's ground was quarried for limestone and ironstone, and a garden can change from rock to clay or fill from one end to the other. We dig trial holes on the visit, because retaining walls on soft fill can move, and a soakaway in the wrong material will not drain.
Can you face the benches in stone to match the village?
We face benches and retaining walls in stone, brick or render to match the house. In the older part of Weldon, a stone finish usually sits best beside the historic buildings; on the newer estates, brick matching the house is often the neater choice. We agree the material with you on the visit.
