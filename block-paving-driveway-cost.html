By council

Planning and dropped kerbs by council

A front driveway needs no planning permission if it is permeable or drains to your own lawn or border; more than 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road does (Planning Portal guidance). In the Stamford conservation area the Article 4 direction brings any new road-facing hard surface under planning control. A new or wider entrance also needs a dropped kerb, and the rules depend on the highway authority.

Questions

Things people ask us

How much does a block paved driveway cost?
A block paved driveway in Rutland and Stamford typically costs £120–£170 per m² all in. A two-car drive of 40–50m² is usually £5,500–£8,500.
What is included in the price of a block paved driveway?
Digging out and taking away the old surface, the sub-base, edging, the finished surface and clearing up. Council fees, such as a dropped kerb, are paid to the council separately.
Why are some block paving quotes much cheaper?
Usually the base. A thin or missing sub-base saves money on day one and fails within a few winters. Ask every contractor to write down the sub-base depth and the product they use.
Is block paving cheaper than a resin driveway?
Usually, yes. Our 2026 guide rates are £120–£170 per m² for block paving against £150–£220 per m² for resin bound, all in, so a two-car drive of 40–50m² is typically £5,500–£8,500 in block. Tarmac is cheaper again at £90–£130 per m², and stabilised gravel cheapest at £60–£100 per m².
How much more do tumbled, premium or permeable blocks cost?
Premium or tumbled blocks add about £15–£35 per m² to our block paving rate, and permeable block paving adds about £20–£40 per m² because of the open-graded base it sits on. Pattern matters too: herringbone involves more cutting than stretcher bond, and a contrasting border adds edge work, so both nudge the price up.
Is the dropped kerb included in the price of a block paved driveway?
The council fee is not; it is paid to the highway authority: £194 in Rutland, £300 or £470 in Stamford, £300 plus a £240 permit in Market Harborough and £333 in Corby, Kettering and Weldon. Building the crossing is quoted separately after the visit, so you can see exactly what the kerb costs on top of the drive.
Does a block paved driveway add value to a house?
Off-street parking is something buyers look for, so a well-built drive generally helps a house sell and can add value where parking is short. What adds nothing is a drive that has sunk, rutted or grown weeds through the joints. Spend the budget on the base and edges first; the blocks are the cheapest part to upgrade.
