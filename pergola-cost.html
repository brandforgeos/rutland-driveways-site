What a louvred pergola costs

What is included in the price

The starting figure covers the powder-coated aluminium frame, the adjustable louvred roof, the gutters built into the frame with water carried away down the posts, and the warm-white LED lighting that comes with it. It also covers fixing the frame down to a base that is already sound and level, and leaving the area tidy.

Before we fix anything we check the base. If the patio is solid we bolt straight to it; if it is not, we allow for concrete pads or a new patio, and that goes on the quote as a separate line so you can see it.

What a louvred pergola costs

What makes it cost more or less

  • Frame size: the bigger the footprint, the more aluminium, louvres and fixings go into it.
  • Manual or motorised louvres, and whether you want them on a remote or an app.
  • Closing the sides with privacy louvres, glass walls, sliding doors or blinds.
  • The state of what it stands on: a sound patio keeps the cost down, a soft or uneven one means pads or a new base.
  • Access to the back garden, as every frame section has to be carried through.
  • Where the rainwater from the posts goes, and whether it needs a new drain or soakaway to take it.

Pergola costs in Rutland, Stamford and the nearby towns

Ground makes the biggest local difference. Around Oakham and in Market Harborough the clay drains slowly, so the water coming down the posts needs a planned route rather than a puddle at the foot of each one, which can add drainage to the job. Stamford and Ketton sit on limestone that usually takes water well. In Corby and Kettering, garden ground can switch from ironstone to clay or fill within a few metres, so we check before fixing. Listed homes, common in Stamford's centre, need planning permission for a pergola, and conservation areas rule out the side-of-house position.

What to check in any quote

  • Whether the base has been looked at, and who is responsible if the patio is not strong enough to fix to.
  • Exactly what size is being priced, and whether the louvres are manual or motorised.
  • Whether lighting and the rainwater route are included or extras.
  • That the frame height has been checked against permitted development limits if it is near a boundary.
  • Which side options are in the price and which are not.

Questions

Things people ask us

What is the cheapest way to get a louvred pergola?
Choose a standard frame size with manual louvres and site it on a patio that is already solid and level. That keeps you close to the from £4,500 starting price for a 3m × 4m frame. Side screens, motors and a new base can always be added later if the frame is set out for them.
Is a louvred pergola worth the money compared with a parasol or awning?
It does a different job. A louvred roof can be opened for sun and air or closed to keep rain off, and the water runs away inside the frame. It stays up all year, has lighting built in and does not need taking down in wind, so the space gets used far more often.
What does a pergola cost to run and look after?
Running costs are small: the LED lighting and, if fitted, the louvre motors. Upkeep is mostly keeping the gutters and louvres clear of leaves and giving the powder-coated aluminium a wash with soapy water now and then. There is no timber to stain or paint.
How do payments work for a pergola?
Everything is set out in your written quote before you commit, including any deposit and when the balance is due. Nothing is payable until you have accepted the quote, and payment details are sent to you at that point rather than on the quote itself.
How long is a pergola quote valid for?
Each written quote states how long the price holds. Aluminium and glass prices move, so if you come back after that date we will check the figures again before booking. The guide prices on this page are for planning your budget, not a fixed offer.
What does the starting price for a louvred pergola include?
The from £4,500 starting price covers a 3m × 4m powder-coated aluminium frame with an adjustable louvred roof, gutters built into the frame that drain down the posts, warm-white LED lighting, fixing it to a sound, level existing base and leaving the area tidy. It does not cover larger sizes, motorised louvres, side screens, glass walls or a new base, which are priced after the site visit and shown as separate lines on the quote.
Does a louvred pergola add value to a house?
It can make a house more appealing to buyers, but it is best thought of as an investment in using your garden rather than a guaranteed return. A covered, lit outdoor space that works in light rain adds useful living space for much of the year, and a tidy aluminium frame on a good patio photographs well for a sale. A pergola on a poor base that has shifted, with sticking louvres, does the opposite.
