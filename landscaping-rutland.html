Rutland gardens

Landscaping across Rutland

England's smallest county has an outsized share of good gardens, from waterside plots around Rutland Water to walled village gardens and rolling paddock-edged lawns. We design and build across all of it: one local team, groundworks to planting, with a written 5 year guarantee.

The county we work in every day

Around the water. The villages ringing Rutland Water — Hambleton, Edith Weston, Empingham, Whitwell, Manton — hold some of the county's most invested-in gardens. Projects here are often complete outdoor living schemes: generous porcelain terraces, lighting, lawns that frame the view rather than compete with it.

The stone villages. Ketton, Exton, Lyddington, Preston and their neighbours set a high bar: limestone walls, Collyweston roofs and gardens that have to look as if they grew there. We lean on natural stone, bound gravel, native planting and restraint.

The market towns and new estates. Oakham, Uppingham and the developments around them bring family gardens — robust lawns, sociable patios, fencing and play space — built to take real life.

  • Full transformations: design, levels, drainage, patios, lawns, boundaries, planting
  • Sleeper terracing and retaining for the county's many sloping plots
  • Materials chosen against local stone, not from a national catalogue
  • Based in Rutland — your site is minutes away, before, during and after the job

Questions

Things people ask us

Which parts of Rutland do you cover?
All of it — the county is twenty minutes corner to corner from our base. Oakham, Uppingham, the Water villages, the A1 corridor villages near Stamford, and everywhere between.
Do you take on large projects?
Complete garden transformations are the heart of our work — multi-week builds covering terracing, patios, lawns, boundaries and planting under one contract and one guarantee.
Can you work alongside our architect or garden designer?
Happily. We price and build from external designs as well as our own, and we will flag anything on the drawings that will not survive contact with Rutland clay.
How much does a garden transformation cost in Rutland?
A full garden transformation in Rutland typically costs £15,000–£35,000, and a refresh, such as a new patio and lawn, £6,000–£12,000. Porcelain patios are £150–£200 per m² and sandstone £130–£180.
