Louvred pergolas in Market Harborough

Louvred pergolas in Market Harborough

A closed louvred roof does exactly what you want in a downpour: it keeps the seating area dry and channels the rain into gutters built inside the frame, then down through the posts. In Market Harborough that last step deserves real thought. The town sits on Jurassic mudstones and clays where the Welland meets the Jordan, the centre flooded from surface water in September 2024, and more than 50 properties flooded in July 2013.

So our visits here start with the ground. We look at where the pergola will stand, test how the soil drains, and decide whether the water from the posts should go to a soakaway, a permeable build-up, or an existing drain. Then we get to the enjoyable part: frame size from 3m × 3m to 8m × 3m, colour, built-in warm-white LED lighting, and whether the sides stay open or close with louvres, glass or blinds.

Market Harborough specifically

What makes Market Harborough different

Mudstone and clay drain slowly, so a soakaway that would work in Stamford may sit full here for days. We test before we design, and where a soakaway will not cope we look at channels or an existing connection. The clay also affects the base: if your patio has been laid thinly over it, we set the posts on concrete pads. In the historic core around the Square, the conservation area rule applies, so a pergola cannot sit between the side of the house and the boundary.

Across the Victorian and Edwardian streets out towards Little Bowden, a typical Market Harborough pergola covers an existing patio behind the house, often 3m × 4m, with LED lighting for evenings. On the Airfield Farm development the patio may be new and builder-laid, so the base check matters, and closed sides are popular for privacy.

Louvred pergolas in Market Harborough: questions

What does a pergola cost in Market Harborough?
Here in Market Harborough a louvred aluminium pergola starts from £4,500 supplied and fitted, for a 3m × 4m frame on a suitable existing base. Drainage work for the roof water, a new base, larger frames, motorised louvres and side screens are priced after the visit, and you then get a fixed written price.
Do I need permission from Harborough District Council?
Not usually for a freestanding rear-garden pergola within permitted development: behind the front of the house, no more than 2.5m at the eaves, 2.5m overall within 2m of a boundary, no platform over 30cm, and under half the garden with outbuildings. Listed houses need permission, and in the conservation area it cannot go beside the house.
Will a pergola make flooding worse in my garden?
The roof collects rain that would otherwise fall across the patio and concentrates it at the posts. Planned properly, that is easier to manage, not harder. We test the ground and send the water to a soakaway, permeable build-up or existing drain, rather than leaving it to pool at the foot of a post.
Can it go on a new patio at Airfield Farm?
Yes, if the patio is sound. Builder-laid patios vary, so we check the bedding where each post will land. If it is solid we fix to it; if not, we cast concrete pads, which also helps on the slow-draining ground here.
