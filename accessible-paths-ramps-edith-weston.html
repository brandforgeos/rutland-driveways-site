Accessible paths & ramps in Edith Weston

Accessible paths & ramps in Edith Weston

Level access is usually thought of as getting in through the front door, but in Edith Weston it is often about getting out into the garden. Terraces, outdoor rooms and lighting designed around the views over Rutland Water are typical projects here, and a step down from a patio door can cut someone off from all of it. We build permanent concrete and paved ramps, level landings and widened paths to the front door, the back door and the terrace.

Edith Weston is a stone village with a green and a number of substantial houses, and it has a conservation area where natural materials are the right choice. A ramp onto a stone terrace should look like part of it rather than an add-on. Gradients and landings are set for the person using them, and Disabled Facilities Grant work follows the occupational therapist's recommendations and is priced to the council's schedule.

Edith Weston specifically

What makes Edith Weston different

Edith Weston is on the limestone plateau above the reservoir, and most plots drain reasonably well, which suits a ramp base. Clay pockets do turn up, and a ramp that crosses one can settle differently from the rest, so we test the ground before designing the base. Rutland's flood strategy does not name Edith Weston for river flooding, but a ramp still needs its run-off sent somewhere sensible, not towards a patio door.

A typical Edith Weston job is a stone or substantial house where a patio or back door has a step down to the terrace. We build a ramp with level landings at the door and the terrace, in a natural finish that matches the paving, with handrails where the rise needs them, often alongside a front door ramp.

Permissions in Edith Weston

  1. PlanningA ramp or path in the garden does not normally need planning permission, but a raised ramp or platform more than 30cm high can. Accessible paths & ramps in Edith Weston: questions

What does a ramp cost in Edith Weston?
Our guide is that a permanent concrete ramp with handrails and a non-slip surface typically costs up to about £2,500. A ramp onto a terrace, with landings and a finish matched to the paving, is priced on the visit, as is any grant-funded work against the council's schedule. The fixed price is always given in writing.
Will a ramp need planning permission in Edith Weston?
For most ramps, no, but terraces are worth a second look: while a garden ramp or path does not normally need permission, a raised ramp or platform more than 30cm high can. Edith Weston has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction. Rutland County Council is the planning authority here.
Can you build a ramp from our patio doors to the terrace?
Gladly. It is one of the most useful adaptations here. We set a level landing at the door, a gentle ramp down to the terrace and a non-slip finish that matches the paving, so the garden and views are reachable every day.
Why test the ground if Edith Weston drains well?
Because clay pockets turn up on the limestone plateau above the reservoir. A ramp sitting partly on clay can move differently from the part on rock, opening cracks or a lip, so we check before designing the base.
