Fencing in Langham

Fencing in Langham

Langham, just north west of Oakham and long known as the home of Ruddles brewery, is one of the Rutland villages with an Article 4 direction on its conservation area. Rutland's directions can cover front walls, gates and fences, so before we design anything facing the road we check what the Langham direction says. Rear and side boundaries between gardens are usually more straightforward. We do that check before any design work starts.

Plots here are often generous, with full rear gardens, so fence runs can be long. Closeboard timber on concrete posts with gravel boards keeps the cost of a long run sensible. Composite and aluminium slatted fencing on aluminium posts suits a screened terrace near the house, and we match gates and screens so the different sections belong together. Because the clay here holds water, we never leave timber touching the soil, and every post is set in concrete at the right depth.

Langham specifically

What makes Langham different

Langham sits on the edge of the Vale of Catmose clay, where water drains slowly, and the Langham Brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity. Rutland's flood strategy names Langham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk. For a fence, that means timber kept up on gravel boards and concrete or aluminium posts set in concrete at the right depth for wet clay. Low corners of a garden need looking at before a solid fence closes them in.

A typical Langham job is a generous rear garden behind a later house, with long side runs that need replacing. It means removing the old fence, setting concrete posts in the clay, fitting gravel boards and closeboard, and sometimes a slatted screen around the patio. Low corners near the brook are checked before the run is closed in.

Questions

Fencing in Langham: questions

What price should we expect for a fence in Langham?
Long Langham runs usually suit closeboard at £80–£100 per metre supplied and fitted on concrete posts. Composite and aluminium slatted fencing is from about £240 per metre fitted at 1.8m high. We price the removal of the existing fence at the visit, and the fixed price is confirmed in writing once the run is measured.
Does the Langham Article 4 direction affect my fence?
It can. Rutland's Article 4 directions can cover front walls, gates and fences, so a road-facing fence in the Langham conservation area may need permission. Elsewhere fences are normally permitted development up to 2m, or 1m next to a road or footpath used by vehicles. We check the direction for your address.
Our garden backs onto the brook. Is a fence still sensible?
Yes, but it needs thought. The Langham Brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity, so we look at levels on the visit, keep timber off the ground and avoid creating a dam in a low corner.
How long should a fence last in Langham clay?
We do not put a number on it, but the things that shorten a fence's life in clay are timber in contact with wet soil and shallow posts. Gravel boards and posts set in concrete at the right depth deal with both.
