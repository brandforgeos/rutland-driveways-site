Getting a dropped kerb approved in Market Harborough

  1. Check the basicsThe parking space should be at least 5.5m deep (4.8m absolute minimum) and 2.4m wide, and steeper than 1:20 over the first 5m is a likely refusal. The county assesses the site within 30 days and decides within two weeks; A, B and C roads need planning permission from the district first.
  2. Planning first if neededYou need planning permission before the kerb application if the house is on a classified (A, B or C) road or is listed.
  3. Apply to the highway authority£300 to apply, then either the county council builds it at a quoted price, or an accredited contractor builds it under a £240 permit. Leicestershire County Council: vehicle access
  4. Build the crossingBuilt by the county council at a quoted price, or an accredited contractor with £10m public liability insurance and an NRSWA street works card, which we can do for you. We hold the NRSWA street works qualification and can build the crossing for you, then build the driveway behind it so the levels line up.

Market Harborough specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Market Harborough

Market Harborough is on Jurassic mudstones and clays, which drain slowly, and the town sits where the River Welland meets the River Jordan. Water management decides most designs here, so we test the ground before choosing a soakaway or a permeable build-up.

The Market Harborough conservation area covers the historic town core and was designated in 1969, with separate conservation areas at Great Bowden and Lubenham. An Article 4 direction served in 2020 on land at Queen's Street, Little Bowden, covers gates, fences and walls.

Dropped kerbs in Market Harborough: questions

How much does a dropped kerb cost in Market Harborough?
Leicestershire County Council charges £300 to apply, then either the county council builds it at a quoted price, or an accredited contractor builds it under a £240 permit. The crossing itself is priced after the site visit.
Who builds the dropped kerb in Market Harborough?
The county council at a quoted price, or an accredited contractor with £10m public liability insurance and an NRSWA street works card, which we can do for you. We can build it under the permit.
How long does a dropped kerb take in Market Harborough?
The county assesses the site within 30 days and decides within two weeks; the approval lasts six months.
Do I need planning permission for a dropped kerb?
Only on a classified (A, B or C) road, for a listed building, or where an Article 4 direction applies. Otherwise the highway authority's consent is what you need.

Planning a dropped kerb in Market Harborough?

We measure up, check levels and drainage, and send a written proposal with the build-up, the programme and one whole price. No pressure and no follow-up sales calls.
