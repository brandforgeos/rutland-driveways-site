Driveway gates in Greetham

Driveway gates in Greetham

Many Greetham drives are being widened for more cars or rebuilt after decades of gravel, and that is often the moment to add gates. A wider opening needs bigger leaves or a longer sliding gate, and stronger posts to carry them. Planning the gate alongside the new drive also means the cable for the motors can go in before the surface is laid, rather than being cut in afterwards. It also lets the gate opening and the new drive edges be set out together, so the posts and the surface finish line up.

The village has a historic core of stone cottages and farm buildings, later infill and estate housing, and Greetham Valley on its edge. Stone-coloured finishes suit the older frontages. We fit sliding and swing gates in aluminium and composite, automate them with keypads, intercoms or remote fobs, and force-test every automated gate to the standard. If you already have good gates, we can usually automate them instead. On estate housing, closing an open front often means building posts from scratch.

Greetham specifically

What makes Greetham different

Greetham sits on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which generally drains well and gives gate posts firm ground to bear on. Rutland's flood strategy does name Greetham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, so on low-lying plots we take more care over footings and keep motors and controls above any likely water. The village has a conservation area but no Article 4 direction, so outside listed buildings, gates follow normal permitted development, and stone-coloured finishes suit the village best.

A typical Greetham job is a house where an old gravel drive is being widened and resurfaced for more cars, and the owners want automated gates fitted as part of the work. It usually involves new posts on concrete footings in the limestone, a ducted cable laid before the new surface, a pair of swing gates or a sliding gate, and photocells.

Questions

Driveway gates in Greetham: questions

What do driveway gates cost in Greetham?
For Greetham, automated swing gates typically cost £4,000–£8,000 supplied and fitted, and a new automated sliding gate £5,000–£10,000. To automate the gates you have now is usually £1,500–£3,000. The power supply is on top, and if the drive is being widened at the same time, ducting the cable early keeps that cost down. A fixed written price follows the visit.
Do I need planning permission for gates in Greetham?
Mostly no. Greetham has no Article 4 direction, so gates are normally permitted development up to 2m high, or 1m next to a road used by vehicles, provided they do not open outwards over the footway or road. Gates facing the road in the conservation area, or on a listed house, can need permission from Rutland County Council.
Should I add gates while my drive is being widened?
It is the best time. The cable for the motors can be ducted under the new surface before it is laid, the posts can be founded properly, and the opening can be sized for the gate from the start. Adding gates later usually means cutting into a finished drive.
Can you automate the old gates we already have?
Often, yes, if they are sound, square and hung on posts that can take the motor. Automating existing gates is usually £1,500–£3,000 plus the power supply. If the posts or hinges are not up to it, we will say so on the visit.
