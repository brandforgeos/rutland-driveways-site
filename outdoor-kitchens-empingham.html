Outdoor kitchens in Empingham

Outdoor kitchens in Empingham

Empingham sits at the eastern end of Rutland Water, below the dam, and many of its gardens look out over water or open country. That makes an outdoor kitchen here as much about where you stand as what you cook on. We lay it out so the cook faces the garden rather than a fence, the grill smoke drifts away from the seating, and the counters give some shelter on an exposed plot.

The build itself is solid: its own concrete base, block or stone-faced counters, weatherproof worktops and the grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge planned in from the start, with the service routes worked out before the concrete goes down. Prices here are guides, and a fixed price comes in writing after the visit, once we have measured how far the kitchen sits from the house. We cover Empingham, Exton, Whitwell, Normanton, Edith Weston and Tickencote.

Empingham specifically

What makes Empingham different

Empingham is on the limestone side of Rutland and much of the village drains reasonably well, but the North Brook runs through it towards the Gwash and ground near the brook stays wet. On a larger plot, the best spot for a view may be some way from the house, and gas, power and water connections are priced on top, so longer runs cost more. The village has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction; natural materials are the safe choice.

Behind a stone house near the church or main street, a typical project is a stone-faced L-shape with a grill, pizza oven and sink, often under a pergola so it can be used in a breeze. On the larger plots at the village edges, the kitchen frequently sits at the far side of a terrace, angled towards the view.

Outdoor kitchens in Empingham: questions

What does an outdoor kitchen in Empingham typically cost?
A built-in run with cabinets, worktop and appliance space starts from about £6,000 in Empingham, and a fully equipped kitchen with grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge is typically £8,000–£20,000. Because gas, power and water connections are priced on top, siting the kitchen far down a large garden does raise the total.
Is planning permission needed for an outdoor kitchen in Empingham?
Usually not, for a kitchen in the back garden. If you add a roof or pergola, it needs to fit the outbuilding height and size limits. Empingham has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, and the planning authority is Rutland County Council.
Should the kitchen go near the house or near the view?
Close to the house keeps service runs short and carrying food easy. Further out may give the better outlook. We show you both on the visit, with the cost difference, and you decide.
How do you deal with wind on an open plot?
We use the counters themselves as shelter, angle the grill so smoke clears the seating, and often suggest a pergola, which is the best way to use an outdoor kitchen in British weather.
