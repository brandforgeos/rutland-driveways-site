Ketton specifically

Homes, ground and conservation in Ketton

Ketton gave its name to Ketton stone, the cream oolitic limestone quarried beside the village, and its older streets are built of it, many under Collyweston slate. Around the historic core are later 20th-century houses and bungalows, and the cement works and quarry sit on the edge of the village.

Ketton is on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which usually drains well, but the River Chater runs through the village to meet the Welland, and ground close to the river is a different matter. We test before designing a soakaway.

Ketton has a conservation area covering the historic village but no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings, with the usual conservation area limits. Ketton stone frontages call for buff and natural tones rather than grey.

The Welland flood warning area at Ketton is the only Environment Agency flood warning area in Rutland, and Rutland's flood strategy names Ketton among the places with a few properties at river flood risk. Check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Rutland conservation areas and Article 4 list · Rutland flood risk strategy.

Rutland County Council

Council rules and fees for Ketton

  • Dropped kerbs: £194 for a Section 184 vehicle access permit (one or two homes); built by an approved contractor with street works accreditation and the right insurance, working under the council's permit. Rutland County Council: dropped kerbs
  • Front driveways: A permeable drive, or one that drains to your own lawn or border, needs no planning permission; more than 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road does. Planning Portal guidance
  • Planning: householder applications cost £548 (national fee from April 2026); pre-application advice from Rutland County Council costs £210. Pre-application advice
  • Building regulations: Leicestershire Building Control Partnership (LBCP). Garage conversion £517.96 building notice (garage conversion up to 36m²); extension £793.09 for a 10–50m² extension. Building control
  • Trees: six weeks' notice for trees in a conservation area, consent for protected trees; both free. Rutland County Council: tree works

Checked October 2026.
