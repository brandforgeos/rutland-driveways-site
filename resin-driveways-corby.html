Resin driveways in Corby

What resin driveways in Corby usually involve

Resin bound is a great fit for Corby homes: a smooth, seamless, permeable driveway that drains freely, needs almost no upkeep and looks crisp against the town's many modern brick-and-render properties. As a Vuba approved installer we lay resin bound driveways across Corby and the surrounding Northamptonshire villages, always on a properly built open-grade base so the surface lasts and stays SuDS-compliant. We also lay block paving, tarmac and bound gravel where they suit the property better.

Why resin bound works so well in Corby

Corby has a lot of modern and newly built housing, and resin bound answers several needs those homes have at once.

It drains. Resin bound is laid over an open-grade permeable base, so rainwater soaks straight through instead of running off. That keeps you on the right side of the rules on paving front gardens, usually without separate soakaways.

It suits modern frontages. Clean grey and buff aggregate blends look sharp against the brick and render common across Corby's estates, where a stark slab or loose gravel can look dated. We bring samples and check them against your frontage in daylight.

It lasts and stays tidy. A seamless resin surface has no joints for weeds, does not scatter like loose gravel or crack like old concrete, and needs little more than an occasional wash.

Built properly, from the base up

A resin driveway is only as good as what sits beneath it. We excavate to the right depth, lay and compact a permeable sub-base and an open-grade binder course, then lay the resin and aggregate — so the finished surface stays sound and free-draining for years. Skipping the base is why cheap resin fails, and we never do.

Corby specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Corby

Corby sits on the Northampton Sand ironstone that fed the steelworks, and large areas around the town were opencast quarried and restored. Ground can change from natural ironstone to clay or fill within a garden, so we dig before we design the base and drainage.

Corby has nine conservation areas, including the Old Village, Cottingham and Middleton, Gretton, Rockingham, Stanion, Great Oakley and Weldon. Its only Article 4 direction (Kingswood) controls houses in multiple occupation and does not affect driveways, porches or extensions.

The Willow Brook runs through Corby and Weldon, with an Environment Agency flood storage reservoir at Weldon, so properties near the brook need drainage planned with care. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: North Northamptonshire conservation areas · Priors Hall Park.
