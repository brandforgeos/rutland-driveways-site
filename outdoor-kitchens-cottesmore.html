Outdoor kitchens in Cottesmore

Outdoor kitchens in Cottesmore

Many Cottesmore homes are family houses where the garden is busy and low maintenance matters. An outdoor kitchen here needs to survive children, dogs and big gatherings, so we favour tough, easy-clean surfaces: weatherproof worktops, block counters faced in stone or render, and doors and appliances placed where they will not be knocked. Everything sits on its own concrete base, not on the patio, so it stays level for years.

The figures on this page are guides, and the fixed price is set in writing after the visit, once we have dug a test hole and measured the runs back to the house for gas, power and water. We work across Cottesmore, from the ironstone and limestone cottages in the old centre to the later family housing, and in Market Overton, Barrow, Burley, Greetham and Thistleton, with Oakham and Langham close by.

Cottesmore specifically

What makes Cottesmore different

Cottesmore sits on mixed ground on the edge of the limestone, and the North Brook rises nearby. Some plots drain freely while others hold water, which matters for a kitchen with a sink and a base that must not move. We dig a test hole and decide between a soakaway and a permeable build-up for the surrounding area. The historic village is a conservation area with no Article 4 direction, so a rear garden kitchen is usually simple from a planning point of view.

On a later family home in the village, the typical outdoor kitchen is an L-shape at the edge of the patio with a grill, a sink and a fridge, faced in render or stone and kept clear of the lawn so it does not get in the way of play. Older cottages in the centre usually suit a smaller stone-faced run.

Outdoor kitchens in Cottesmore: questions

How much is an outdoor kitchen in Cottesmore?
A built-in kitchen with cabinets, worktop and appliance space in Cottesmore starts from about £6,000. A family-sized setup with grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge is typically £8,000–£20,000, with gas, power and water connections priced on top. We put the fixed price in writing once we have seen the garden.
Will I need planning for an outdoor kitchen in Cottesmore?
Not normally, as long as it is in the rear garden. Should you want a roof or pergola over it, that has to meet the outbuilding height and size limits. The historic village is a conservation area, there is no Article 4 direction, and Rutland County Council deals with any application.
Is an outdoor kitchen safe with young children around?
We plan the layout so the grill sits away from the main play space and walkways, with worktop either side for setting things down. Gas work is done by a Gas Safe engineer and new circuits by a registered electrician.
Do I need a gas supply, or can I use bottles?
No. Many grills and pizza ovens run on bottled gas, charcoal or wood. A fixed supply is tidier and saves changing bottles, but it must be fitted by a Gas Safe engineer and is priced on top of the kitchen.
