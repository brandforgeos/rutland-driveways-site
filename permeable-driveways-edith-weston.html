Permeable driveways in Edith Weston

Permeable driveways in Edith Weston

Edith Weston sits on the south shore of Rutland Water, a stone village with a green and a number of substantial houses, next to St George's Barracks. In a setting like that, a driveway has to look right as well as drain. A permeable driveway lets rain soak through the surface and an open-graded base into the ground. We build them in resin bound, permeable block paving and stabilised gravel, and in the conservation area we steer towards natural-looking materials.

The village is on the limestone plateau above the reservoir, and most plots drain reasonably well, which is good ground for a permeable system. Clay pockets do turn up, and a drive laid over one without checking can hold water in its base. We test the ground before designing, then size the sub-base and decide whether any part needs lining. Your fixed written price follows once we know what the plot is like.

Edith Weston specifically

What makes Edith Weston different

Edith Weston has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings, with natural materials the right choice in the conservation area. Flood risk is not the driver here that it is in some Rutland villages: Rutland Water itself carries negligible flood risk according to Rutland's flood strategy, and Edith Weston is not one of the places it names for river flooding. The ground, and those clay pockets, are what decide the design.

A typical Edith Weston job is a larger drive serving one of the substantial stone houses, or a frontage near the green being replaced with something more in keeping. We test the limestone, check for clay, and build an open-graded base, then finish in a natural-toned resin bound, stabilised gravel or permeable block chosen to sit with the stone.

Permeable driveways in Edith Weston: questions

What will a permeable drive cost in Edith Weston?
As a guide, expect £150–£220 per m² for permeable resin bound, £140–£210 per m² for permeable block paving and £60–£100 per m² for stabilised gravel, all in. Most plots on the limestone can soak away through the base, but if a clay pocket needs lining that adds to the price. The fixed figure comes in writing after the visit.
Do I need planning permission for a new drive in Edith Weston?
Edith Weston has no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development applies outside listed buildings. A permeable new or replacement front drive needs no planning permission, nor does one that drains to a lawn or border on your own land. Natural materials are the right choice in the conservation area.
What are clay pockets and why do they matter?
Edith Weston's limestone generally drains, but patches of clay turn up within it. If a permeable base sits on one, water can collect there instead of soaking away. A test hole shows whether your drive is affected, and if it is, we can line that part and take the water to a controlled outlet.
Which finish suits a stone house near the green?
In the conservation area, natural materials are the right choice, so we usually suggest a natural-toned stabilised gravel, resin bound or a permeable block in a stone colour rather than grey concrete. We look at your house and its neighbours on the visit before recommending one.
