Driveway gates in Kettering: questions

How much does a driveway gate cost in Kettering?
A new automated sliding gate typically costs £5,000–£10,000 supplied and fitted, and automated swing gates £4,000–£8,000. Automating gates you already have is usually £1,500–£3,000. The power supply is priced on top. Your fixed price comes in writing after the site visit.
Do I need planning permission for a driveway gate in Kettering?
Gates are normally permitted development up to 2m high, or 1m next to a road used by vehicles. They must not open outwards over the footway or road. In a conservation area or on a listed house, gates facing the road can need permission. Listed buildings and the Kettering conservation area have tighter rules, so we check your address before anything is designed.
Does the ground in Kettering make a difference?
Ground in Kettering can change within a single garden, so we dig the first post hole as a trial and set the depth of every footing from what we find.
Sliding or swing gates?
Sliding gates suit drives that slope up from the road or are short, because they do not need space to swing. Swing gates are simpler where there is room for them to open inwards.
Are electric gates safe?
They are when they are fitted with safety edges or photocells and force-tested after installation, which we do on every automated gate.
