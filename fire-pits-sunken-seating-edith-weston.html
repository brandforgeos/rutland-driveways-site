Fire pits & sunken seating in Edith Weston

On the south shore of Rutland Water, Edith Weston's gardens are often planned around the view and the evening light. A fire pit extends that, giving you somewhere to stay out as the light goes and the air cools. Terraces, outdoor rooms and lighting are typical projects here, and a built-in pit or sunken seating area often forms part of a wider garden build. We build both, with retaining walls, benches, steps and drainage. Prices given are guides, fixed in writing after we visit.

The village is stone-built, with a green and a number of substantial houses, next to St George's Barracks. Larger properties tend to have terraces stepping down the garden, which suits seating built into a change of level, while smaller cottages may have room for a compact pit on the patio. We also cover North Luffenham, South Luffenham, Lyndon, Manton and Normanton, and plan each layout around its own garden. Views differ from plot to plot too.

Edith Weston specifically

What makes Edith Weston different

Edith Weston stands on the limestone plateau above the reservoir, and most plots drain reasonably well. Clay pockets do turn up, and a sunken seating area dug into one will hold water where the rest of the garden does not, so we test the ground before designing the base and drainage. Rutland Water itself carries negligible flood risk according to Rutland's flood strategy, and Edith Weston is not one of the places it names for river flooding. Natural materials suit the conservation area.

A typical Edith Weston job is a substantial stone house with a terrace looking towards the water. We build the seating into the change of level below the terrace, with a retaining wall forming the bench back, steps down at one end and a lined fire pit at the centre. Low lighting along the steps and bench edges often completes it.

Fire pits & sunken seating in Edith Weston: questions

How much is a fire pit with seating in Edith Weston?
In Edith Weston, a fire pit with built-in seating costs from about £3,500, and a built-in stone or brick fire pit typically £1,200–£2,000. A full sunken seating area with retaining walls and drainage is priced per job, and in Edith Weston it is often part of a wider terrace or garden build. The written price follows the visit.
Do I need planning permission in Edith Weston?
Edith Weston follows the general rule: a fire pit or sunken seating area in a rear garden does not normally need planning permission. Edith Weston has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings, with natural materials the right choice. Wherever it sits, keep the fire a safe distance from fences, trees and buildings.
Can the fire pit area work with garden lighting?
Yes. Lighting is a common part of garden projects here, and it makes steps and bench edges easier to use after dark. We plan any cable routes before the walls and paving go down, so nothing has to be lifted later to add it.
Our garden drains well. Why test it?
Because the limestone here has clay pockets, and you cannot see them from the surface. A sunken area that happens to land on one will hold water while the lawn around it stays dry. A test hole on the visit tells us which we are dealing with before the drainage is designed.
