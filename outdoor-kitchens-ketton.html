Outdoor kitchens in Ketton

Outdoor kitchens in Ketton

Ketton gave its name to a building stone, and its older houses are faced in that cream oolitic limestone, many under Collyweston slate. An outdoor kitchen faced in cold grey would look out of place in those gardens, so we choose stone or render in buff and natural tones. Underneath, it is a proper build: a concrete base, block counters and weatherproof worktops, with the grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge planned in.

Our figures are guides; the fixed price comes in writing after the visit, once we have seen the garden, the access past the house and how far the gas, power and water have to travel. Near the river we also want to see how wet the ground is before we commit to a base design. We cover Ketton and nearby Tinwell, Collyweston, Barrowden, Tixover, Aldgate and Geeston, along with Stamford, Ryhall and Empingham.

Ketton specifically

What makes Ketton different

Ketton is on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which usually drains well, but the River Chater runs through the village to the Welland, and ground close to it behaves differently. The Welland flood warning area at Ketton is the only Environment Agency flood warning area in Rutland. For a kitchen, that affects where the base goes and how high it sits. The historic village is a conservation area with no Article 4 direction, so a rear garden kitchen is normally straightforward.

Behind a stone cottage in the old village, the typical kitchen is modest: a short run of buff-faced counters with a grill or pizza oven against a garden wall. Behind the later 20th-century houses and bungalows on the edges, gardens are often more open, and an L-shaped kitchen with a sink and fridge beside a patio is a common choice.

Sources: Rutland conservation areas and Article 4 list · Rutland flood risk strategy.

Outdoor kitchens in Ketton: questions

What will an outdoor kitchen cost in Ketton?
A Ketton kitchen with built-in counters, a worktop and space for appliances starts from about £6,000. Fully kitted with grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge, it is typically £8,000–£20,000. Gas, power and water connections are priced on top of the kitchen itself. Stone facing in buff tones is included in the fixed price we put in writing after visiting.
Does the Ketton conservation area mean I need planning?
Not normally for a kitchen in a rear garden. Ketton has a conservation area but no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development applies outside listed buildings, with the usual conservation area limits. Cover over the kitchen, such as a pergola, has to stay within the outbuilding height and size limits. Applications are decided by Rutland County Council.
We are near the River Chater. Is a kitchen still possible?
Usually, yes. Ground near the river is wetter than the limestone elsewhere in the village, so we test it, think about the base height and keep the fridge and electrics on the higher part of the layout.
Can you match the colour of a Ketton stone house?
We aim for faces in buff and natural tones that sit comfortably with the cream stone, and we bring samples to compare against your walls rather than choosing from a picture.
