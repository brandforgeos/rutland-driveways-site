Ground and conservation in Oakham

Oakham sits in the Vale of Catmose, and the ground around much of the town is heavy clay that holds water through the winter. Base depth and drainage are where Oakham drives succeed or fail, so we dig a test hole and design the soakaway, channel or permeable build-up on the visit. Digging down into the clay in Oakham makes a sump that collects water, so a drained floor and drainage behind every retaining wall are not optional here; where there is nowhere for it to go, we pipe it to a lower outlet.

The Oakham conservation area covers the historic centre, but Oakham has no Article 4 direction, so outside listed buildings the normal permitted development rules apply, with the usual conservation area limits.

Oakham is listed in Rutland County Council's flood strategy as a surface water flood risk area of national significance, and the Barleythorpe Brook runs culverted under parts of the town. Water that has nowhere to go is the commonest problem we fix here. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Rutland conservation areas · Rutland flood risk strategy.

Fire pits & sunken seating in Oakham: questions

How much does a fire pit cost in Oakham?
A built-in stone or brick fire pit typically costs £1,200–£2,000, and a fire pit with built-in seating from about £3,500. A full sunken seating area with retaining walls and drainage is priced per job, usually as part of a wider garden build. Your fixed price comes in writing after the site visit.
Do I need planning permission for a fire pit in Oakham?
A fire pit or sunken seating area in a rear garden does not normally need planning permission. Keep fires a safe distance from fences, trees and buildings, and be considerate of neighbours: smoke can be a statutory nuisance. Listed buildings and the Oakham conservation area have tighter rules, so we check your address before anything is designed.
Does the ground in Oakham make a difference?
Digging down into the clay in Oakham makes a sump that collects water, so a drained floor and drainage behind every retaining wall are not optional here; where there is nowhere for it to go, we pipe it to a lower outlet.
Will a sunken seating area fill with water?
Not if it is drained. We put a drain or soakaway at the low point, sized for the ground, before the paving goes down.
Gas or wood fire pit?
Wood gives the real fire and the smell; gas is cleaner and turns on at a switch. A gas pit needs a Gas Safe engineer.
