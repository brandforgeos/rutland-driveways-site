House extensions in Kettering

What house extensions in Kettering usually involve

Extending is the most cost-effective way to gain space and value without the upheaval of moving. We design and build single and double storey extensions, kitchen and dining extensions and rear and side returns across Kettering and the surrounding Northamptonshire villages — matched to the town's red-brick Victorian houses and its modern estates alike. Every build meets Building Regulations and is signed off through Local Authority Building Control, with the planning or permitted-development route confirmed before we start.

The extensions Kettering homes ask for most

Kettering's housing runs from Victorian terraces to modern estates, and the right extension depends on the age and style of the property.

Victorian and Edwardian terraces near the centre often suit a rear or side-return extension to open a narrow kitchen into a single bright family space. This typically means removing a load-bearing wall and installing a steel beam to a structural engineer's calculations, which we do as standard.

Red-brick and ironstone period homes need matched brick and sympathetic detailing, and where a conservation area applies we prepare the work to the standard a conservation officer expects.

Modern estate homes around Barton Seagrave and the edges of Kettering frequently allow rear extensions under permitted development, avoiding a full planning application. We confirm the route at the first visit.

What Building Regulations cover

Every extension must meet Building Regulations regardless of planning: foundations sized for the ground, a damp proof course tied into the existing wall, insulation to current standards, structural support over openings, fire safety, ventilation and drainage. We build to those standards and arrange Building Control inspections at foundations, damp proofing, structure and completion — so you receive the completion certificate a buyer's solicitor will later want.

Ground, drainage and conservation in Kettering

Ground around Kettering ranges from ironstone to clay, and new-build plots are often compacted subsoil under a thin layer of topsoil. We check what is under each garden before choosing the base and the drainage.

Kettering has 26 conservation areas, including the town centre, Barton Seagrave, Burton Latimer, Geddington and Rothwell. Its Article 4 directions are site-specific, mostly older ones covering individual streets or buildings, so we check your address before designing anything that faces the road.

The River Ise runs along the east of the town, with river level stations at Barford Bridge and Slade Brook. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: North Northamptonshire Article 4 directions · Hanwood Park.

House extensions in Kettering: questions

How long does an extension take?
Single-storey extensions usually run eight to fourteen weeks on site once approvals are in place; double-storey work takes longer. We give a realistic written programme.
Is the work properly certified?
Yes — built to Building Regulations, inspected by Building Control at the key stages, and issued with a completion certificate.
Can you match the existing brick?
In most cases yes. We source matched or closely blended brick and roofing so the extension reads as part of the original house.
How much does a house extension cost in Kettering?
A single storey extension in Kettering typically costs £2,400–£3,200 per m² finished ready for decoration, roughly £29,000–£38,500 for a 12m² rear extension, with any kitchen fit-out priced separately. See our price guides. Your fixed price comes in writing after the site visit.
Do I need planning permission for a house extension in Kettering?
Many single storey rear extensions are permitted development; a householder application costs £548 and a lawful development certificate £274 (national fees from April 2026).
Who handles building control in Kettering?
North Northamptonshire Council building control. The fee is quoted by the council for each job. We make the application and arrange the inspections as part of the job.
