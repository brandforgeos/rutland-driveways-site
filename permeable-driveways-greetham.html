Permeable driveways in Greetham

Permeable driveways in Greetham

Many drives in Greetham are being widened for more cars or replaced after decades of gravel. Loose gravel already lets water through, so moving to a permeable surface keeps that benefit while giving a firmer finish. Stabilised gravel holds the stones in place, permeable block paving gives a crisp edge, and permeable resin bound gives a smooth surface. All three are built on an open-graded base so rain soaks through into the ground rather than running to the road.

The village sits just west of the A1 on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which generally drains well, so on most plots a permeable base can let water soak away naturally. Rutland's flood strategy does name Greetham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, so low-lying plots need more thought. Rutland Driveways & Landscaping assesses the ground on every visit and follows it with a fixed written price.

Greetham specifically

What makes Greetham different

Greetham has a historic core of stone cottages and farm buildings, later infill and estate housing, and Greetham Valley on its edge. There is a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, so outside listed buildings front driveways and walls follow normal permitted development, and stone-coloured materials suit the village. For a permeable drive, that points towards natural gravel tones, buff resin bound or warm-toned block rather than grey concrete. Rutland County Council handles planning and highways.

A typical Greetham job is lifting a worn gravel drive on a later infill house, widening it for an extra car, and rebuilding it as stabilised gravel or resin bound on a proper open-graded base. On the limestone, the water usually soaks away through the base. On a lower-lying plot we test first and allow for a lined option if needed.

Questions

Permeable driveways in Greetham: questions

What does a permeable drive cost in Greetham?
We quote guide prices of £150–£220 per m² for permeable resin bound, £140–£210 per m² for permeable block paving and £60–£100 per m² for stabilised gravel, all in. On Greetham's limestone, the base can usually soak away without lining, and the fixed price comes in writing after the visit.
Will I need planning permission to replace my Greetham drive?
Greetham has no Article 4 direction, so outside listed buildings front driveways follow normal permitted development. A permeable new or replacement front drive needs no planning permission, and nor does one whose water drains to a lawn or border on your own land. Stone-coloured materials suit the conservation area.
Why replace a gravel drive that already drains?
Loose gravel lets water through, but it scatters, ruts and gets carried indoors over the years. A permeable stabilised gravel, block or resin bound drive keeps the drainage while giving a firm surface. Built on an open-graded base, it also deals properly with the ground beneath rather than relying on whatever was laid decades ago.
Does flood risk affect permeable drives in Greetham?
On most of the village's free-draining limestone, the ground usually does the work. Rutland's flood strategy names Greetham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, though, and for low-lying plots we look harder at the ground and the water route before designing.
