Driveway drainage in Kettering

Driveway drainage in Kettering

Kettering's ground ranges from ironstone to clay, and on the newer estates there is an extra problem: plots are often compacted subsoil under a thin layer of topsoil. That combination looks fine on handover and then holds every drop of rain. When the drive falls towards the lawn, the lawn turns to mud; when it falls towards the house, water sits at the door. We check what is under each garden before choosing a channel position, a soakaway or a crate system.

The town's older housing brings different issues. Victorian and Edwardian terraces and villas close to the centre and along the Headlands often have short frontages with little room for a soakaway, and drives that have been relaid more than once. Interwar and post-war estates, Barton Seagrave to the south east and Hanwood Park on the east side, planned for up to 5,500 homes, each have their own typical layout, from narrow single drives to wider shared frontages.

Kettering specifically

What makes Kettering different

The thing we most often find in Kettering is ground that will not take water: compacted subsoil on new-build plots, or clay on older ones. A percolation test settles whether a soakaway is realistic or whether a crate system or permeable area makes more sense. Where the ground is ironstone, things are usually easier. The River Ise runs along the east of the town. Kettering has 26 conservation areas with site-specific Article 4 directions, mostly covering individual streets or buildings, so we check your address before anything that faces the road is changed.

A typical Kettering job is a newer house with a block drive that drains towards a lawn that never dries out, or an older semi where water pools at the garage. We fit a heavy-duty channel across the low point, take the water to a soakaway or crate system sized from a percolation test, and correct the falls where the base has settled.

You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Questions

Driveway drainage in Kettering: questions

What is the cost of driveway drainage in Kettering?
For Kettering, the guide is about £1,000–£1,800 installed for a channel drain across a typical driveway or garage entrance, or £120–£200 per metre for a heavy-duty, vehicle-rated channel. Because so many plots here are compacted, soakaways are sized and priced after a percolation test. The fixed price is confirmed in writing after the visit.
Will I need planning permission for drainage in Kettering?
Drainage on your own Kettering driveway needs no planning permission in itself. The town's Article 4 directions are site-specific, so if the work involves changing the drive surface or boundary on a road frontage we check your address first. Whatever the address, drive water must stay out of the foul sewer and off the road.
Why is the lawn next to our new drive always waterlogged?
New-build gardens around Kettering are often compacted subsoil under a thin topsoil, so water running off the drive cannot soak in and spreads across the grass. A channel at the edge of the drive, piped to a properly sized soakaway or crate system, gives that water somewhere to go.
Our terrace has a tiny frontage. Is there room for drainage?
Usually there is room for a short heavy-duty channel across the front, and the question is where the water goes next. On a small Kettering frontage we look at compact crate systems, permeable areas and existing surface water connections before deciding, and never connect to the foul sewer.
