Louvred pergolas in Weldon

What louvred pergolas in Weldon usually involve

A louvred pergola is a freestanding powder-coated aluminium frame with a roof of adjustable louvres. Open them for sun and air; close them and the roof sheds rain into gutters inside the frame and down through the posts. Lighting is built in, and the sides can be left open or closed in with screens, glass or blinds.

How much does a pergola cost in Weldon?

Do I need planning permission for a pergola in Weldon?
A freestanding pergola in a rear garden is normally permitted development if it stays behind the front of the house, is no more than 2.5m high at the eaves and 2.5m overall within 2m of a boundary, does not sit on a platform more than 30cm high, and together with any sheds or other outbuildings covers no more than half the garden. Will my patio take a pergola in Weldon?
Ground in Weldon can change from ironstone to clay within one garden, so we dig at the post positions first and choose between fixing to the existing patio, concrete pads or a new base from what we find.
How long does a pergola take to fit?
The frame and roof usually go up in a day once the base is ready. If we are building concrete pads or a new patio first, allow a few extra days for that and for the concrete to go off.
Does a pergola need power?
Only for the lights and motorised louvres. They run on low voltage from an outdoor supply, which a registered electrician puts in and certifies. Manual louvres need no power at all.
Do I need building regulations for a pergola?
Not for an open-sided pergola. If you want all four sides closed in with glass, that can change, so we check it at the site visit.
