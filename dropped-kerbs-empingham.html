Getting a dropped kerb approved in Empingham

  1. Check the basicsYou need the highways team's consent before anything touches the verge or footway. A frontage onto a classified (A, B or C) road, a listed building or anything other than a single house also needs planning permission first.
  2. Planning first if neededYou need planning permission before the kerb application if the house is on a classified (A, B or C) road or is listed.
  3. Apply to the highway authority£194 for a section 184 vehicle access permit (one or two homes). Rutland County Council: dropped kerbs
  4. Build the crossingBuilt by an approved contractor with street works accreditation and the right insurance, working under the council's permit. We hold the NRSWA street works qualification and can build the crossing for you, then build the driveway behind it so the levels line up.
  5. Build the drivewayThe drive behind the kerb is built to drain to your own ground, so no water runs onto the road.

Empingham specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Empingham

Empingham is on the limestone side of Rutland, and much of the village drains reasonably well, but the North Brook runs through it on its way to the Gwash, and ground near the brook stays wet.

Empingham has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction. Front-garden work outside listed buildings follows normal permitted development, with natural materials the safe choice in the conservation area.

Dropped kerbs in Empingham: questions

How much does a dropped kerb cost in Empingham?
Rutland County Council charges £194 for a Section 184 vehicle access permit (one or two homes). The crossing itself is priced after the site visit.
Who builds the dropped kerb in Empingham?
An approved contractor with street works accreditation and the right insurance, working under the council's permit. We can build it under the permit.
How long does a dropped kerb take in Empingham?
Timing depends on the council's highways team; we confirm it at the site visit and plan the driveway around it.
Do I need planning permission for a dropped kerb?
Only on a classified (A, B or C) road, for a listed building, or where an Article 4 direction applies. Otherwise the highway authority's consent is what you need.

Planning a dropped kerb in Empingham?

We measure up, check levels and drainage, and send a written proposal with the build-up, the programme and one whole price. No pressure and no follow-up sales calls.
