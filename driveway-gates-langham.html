Driveway gates in Langham

Driveway gates in Langham

Langham, just north west of Oakham and long known as the home of Ruddles brewery, is one of the Rutland villages with an Article 4 direction on its conservation area. Rutland's Article 4 directions can cover front walls, gates and fences, so new gates facing the road may need planning permission even where they would otherwise be permitted development. We check the Langham direction against your address before designing anything.

Plots here are often generous, with sweeping drives in front of ironstone and limestone houses in the centre and later homes around them. On a long drive, gates can be set well back from the road, which gives room for swing leaves to open inwards. We fit sliding and swing gates in aluminium and composite, automate them, and force-test every automated gate to the standard after installation. A long drive also means planning the power run and the intercom together.

Langham specifically

What makes Langham different

Langham sits on the edge of the Vale of Catmose clay, which drains slowly and moves with the seasons, so gate posts need deeper footings than on limestone. The Langham Brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity, and Rutland's flood strategy names the village among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, so near the brook we keep motors and the control box out of low spots. Combined with the Article 4 direction, that makes Langham a village where gates are worth designing carefully.

A typical Langham job is a detached house with a sweeping drive, where the owners want automated swing gates set back from the road with an intercom for visitors. It usually starts with an Article 4 check, then involves posts on deep footings in clay, a cable run from the house, the gates themselves, and photocells and safety edges across the opening.

Driveway gates in Langham: questions

How much do automated gates cost in Langham?
In Langham, a new automated sliding gate typically costs £5,000–£10,000 supplied and fitted, and automated swing gates £4,000–£8,000. Automating gates already hung is usually £1,500–£3,000. Power supply is extra. If an application is needed under the Article 4 direction, we tell you before you commit, and the fixed price is confirmed in writing after the visit.
Does Langham's Article 4 direction affect gates?
It may. Langham's conservation area has an Article 4 direction, and Rutland's directions can cover front walls, gates and fences. If it applies to your property, road-facing gates need planning permission from Rutland County Council. We check the direction for your address before designing anything facing the road.
Will Langham's clay make my gate posts lean?
Not if the footings suit the ground. Vale clay holds water and moves with wet and dry seasons, which can tilt a shallow post over time. We dig to see the ground and design footings deep and wide enough for the clay, the gate and the motor.
We are near the Langham Brook. Anything to watch?
There is. The brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity, so we keep the control box and connections as high as practical and avoid motor positions at the lowest point. It is also worth checking how water leaves the drive.
