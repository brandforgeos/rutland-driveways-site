Porcelain patios in Market Harborough

What porcelain patios in Market Harborough usually involve

A well-built patio turns a tired back garden into a proper outdoor room. We design and lay porcelain, natural stone and sandstone patios across Market Harborough and the surrounding Leicestershire villages, on correctly built bases with the falls and drainage that stop puddling and movement. Whether you want a crisp contemporary porcelain terrace or a softer natural-stone look to suit a period home, we build it to last, not just to look good on day one.

Patios built for Market Harborough gardens

The right patio depends on the house and how you want to use the garden, and Market Harborough has plenty of variety.

Porcelain has become the first choice for many homeowners — it is dense, frost-proof, barely fades, resists stains and needs very little maintenance. It suits modern brick-and-render homes especially well, and we lay it on a full mortar bed with the correct priming so it stays put.

Natural stone and sandstone give a warmer, more traditional look that sits beautifully against period brick and older cottages around the town and villages. Each is laid to proper falls so water runs away from the house, not toward it.

The base is everything. A patio that lifts, rocks or ponds was almost always laid on a poor base. We dig out properly, lay and compact a sub-base, and bed every slab fully — the unglamorous work that decides whether a patio lasts five years or twenty-five.

Patios and the rest of the garden

Because we landscape as well as pave, a patio can be planned together with steps, retaining walls, lighting, planting and lawn — so levels and drainage line up and the finished garden works as one, rather than a patio dropped into an afterthought.

Ground, drainage and conservation in Market Harborough

Market Harborough is on Jurassic mudstones and clays, which drain slowly, and the town sits where the River Welland meets the River Jordan. Water management decides most designs here, so we test the ground before choosing a soakaway or a permeable build-up.

The Market Harborough conservation area covers the historic town core and was designated in 1969, with separate conservation areas at Great Bowden and Lubenham. An Article 4 direction served in 2020 on land at Queen's Street, Little Bowden, covers gates, fences and walls.

The town centre flooded from surface water in September 2024, when a high River Welland stopped the drains discharging, and more than 50 properties flooded in July 2013. Permeable surfaces and attenuation are worth taking seriously here. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Market Harborough flood report, Sept 2024 · Market Harborough conservation area.

Porcelain patios in Market Harborough: questions

Porcelain or natural stone — which is better?
Neither is simply better; they suit different homes. Porcelain is lower-maintenance, stain-resistant and crisp, ideal for modern homes. Natural stone is warmer and more traditional, suiting period properties. We will show you samples against your house and garden.
Will the patio drain properly?
Yes. Every patio is laid to a slight fall away from the house so surface water runs off, with drainage channels where needed. Poor falls are the main cause of puddling, and we get them right.
Do you move or build up levels?
We can. Split-level gardens, raised patios and steps are routine — we build retaining walls and steps as part of the job where the levels need it.
How long does a patio take?
Most patios take one to two weeks depending on size, access and any groundwork or steps involved. We give a clear programme up front.
