House extensions in Market Harborough

What house extensions in Market Harborough usually involve

Extending is the most cost-effective way to gain space and value without leaving a town you like. We design and build single and double storey extensions, kitchen and dining extensions and rear and side returns across Market Harborough and the surrounding Leicestershire villages — matched to everything from red-brick Victorian terraces near the centre to modern estate homes on the edges. Every build meets Building Regulations and is signed off through Local Authority Building Control, with the planning or permitted-development route confirmed before we start.

The extensions Market Harborough homes ask for most

Market Harborough's housing runs across every era, and the right extension depends on the age and style of the property.

Period brick homes near the town centre and in the conservation area need extensions in matched brick with sympathetic detailing and appropriate roofing. Planning permission is usually required here, and we prepare the work to the standard a conservation officer expects.

Victorian and Edwardian houses often suit a rear or side-return extension to open a cramped kitchen into a single bright family space — typically removing a load-bearing wall and installing a steel beam to a structural engineer's calculations, which we do as standard.

Modern estate homes around the edges of Market Harborough frequently allow rear extensions under permitted development, avoiding a full planning application. We confirm the route at the first visit so there are no surprises.

What Building Regulations cover

Every extension must meet Building Regulations regardless of planning: foundations sized for the ground, a damp proof course tied into the existing wall, insulation to current standards, structural support over openings, fire safety, ventilation and drainage. We build to those standards and arrange Building Control inspections at foundations, damp proofing, structure and completion — so you receive the completion certificate a buyer's solicitor will later want.

Ground, drainage and conservation in Market Harborough

Market Harborough is on Jurassic mudstones and clays, which drain slowly, and the town sits where the River Welland meets the River Jordan. Water management decides most designs here, so we test the ground before choosing a soakaway or a permeable build-up.

The Market Harborough conservation area covers the historic town core and was designated in 1969, with separate conservation areas at Great Bowden and Lubenham. An Article 4 direction served in 2020 on land at Queen's Street, Little Bowden, covers gates, fences and walls.

The town centre flooded from surface water in September 2024, when a high River Welland stopped the drains discharging, and more than 50 properties flooded in July 2013. Permeable surfaces and attenuation are worth taking seriously here. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Market Harborough flood report, Sept 2024 · Market Harborough conservation area.

House extensions in Market Harborough: questions

How long does an extension take?
Single-storey extensions usually run eight to fourteen weeks on site once approvals are in place; double-storey work takes longer. We give a realistic written programme, not an optimistic one.
Is the work properly certified?
Yes — built to Building Regulations, inspected by Building Control at the key stages, and issued with a completion certificate at the end.
Can you match the existing brick?
In most cases yes. We source matched or closely blended brick and roofing so the extension reads as part of the original house.
How much does a house extension cost in Market Harborough?
A single storey extension in Market Harborough typically costs £2,400–£3,200 per m² finished ready for decoration, roughly £29,000–£38,500 for a 12m² rear extension, with any kitchen fit-out priced separately. See our price guides. Your fixed price comes in writing after the site visit.
Do I need planning permission for a house extension in Market Harborough?
Many single storey rear extensions are permitted development; a householder application costs £548 and a lawful development certificate £274 (national fees from April 2026).
Who handles building control in Market Harborough?
Leicestershire Building Control Partnership (LBCP). The fee is £793.09 for a 10–50m² extension. We make the application and arrange the inspections as part of the job.
