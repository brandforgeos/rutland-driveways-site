Landscaping in Langham

Landscaping in Langham: what is involved

A garden transformation takes a garden from an idea to a finished space under one contract: levels, drainage, paving, walls, steps, beds, lawn, fencing and lighting, built in the right order.

Langham is a large village just north west of Oakham, long known as the home of Ruddles brewery, with ironstone and limestone houses in the centre and later housing around it. Plots are often generous enough for sweeping drives and full rear gardens.

We cover Langham (LE15) and the villages around it, including Barleythorpe, Ashwell, Whissendine, Cold Overton and Knossington.

Langham specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Langham

Langham sits on the edge of the Vale of Catmose clay, where water drains slowly, and its brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity. Base depth and drainage decide most designs here.

Langham has a conservation area with an Article 4 direction. Rutland's Article 4 directions can cover front walls, gates and fences, parking spaces, porches and roofs, so we check the Langham direction before designing anything facing the road.

Rutland's flood strategy notes that the Langham Brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity, and names Langham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Rutland conservation areas and Article 4 list · Rutland flood risk strategy.

Permissions and fees in Langham

  1. PlanningLangham has an Article 4 direction, which can bring front walls, gates, parking spaces and porches under planning control, so we check it before designing anything that faces the road. Most garden work is permitted development. Walls, fences and gates over 1m beside a road (2m elsewhere), raised platforms and work to listed buildings or protected trees are the usual exceptions.
