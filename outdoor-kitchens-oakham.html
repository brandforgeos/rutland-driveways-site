Outdoor kitchens in Oakham

Outdoor kitchens in Oakham

A cooking area in the garden gets used hard: hot pans, spilled oil, a fridge running all summer and people standing around it in all weathers. In Oakham we treat it as a small building rather than a piece of furniture. That means a concrete base of its own, block or stone-faced counters, weatherproof worktops, and the grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge positioned from the start so that nothing has to be squeezed in later.

Prices here are guide figures until we have seen the garden. The fixed price comes in writing after the visit, once we know how far the gas, power and water have to travel from the house, whether the side access is wide enough for materials, and what the ground is like under the lawn or patio. We cover the whole town, from the old streets near the castle and the Market Place to Barleythorpe and Oakham Heights, plus villages such as Langham, Braunston-in-Rutland, Egleton, Burley and Whissendine.

Oakham specifically

What makes Oakham different

Much of Oakham stands on heavy Vale of Catmose clay that stays wet through the winter, and Rutland County Council's flood strategy lists the town as a surface water flood risk area of national significance. For an outdoor kitchen that changes two things. The base has to go deep enough to stay stable as the clay swells and shrinks, and the water coming off the worktops, the sink and any roof over it needs somewhere to go. We dig a test hole on the visit and design that route before choosing finishes.

On an interwar semi off Brooke Road or Burley Road, the usual setup is an L-shaped run of counters against a rear boundary or the side of the house, with a grill, a small sink and an under-counter fridge. On the newer Barleythorpe plots, gardens are smaller and the ground is often compacted fill, so a straight run with a pizza oven at one end is common.

Outdoor kitchens in Oakham: questions

What does an outdoor kitchen cost in Oakham?
A built-in Oakham kitchen with counters, worktop and appliance space starts from about £6,000. Add a grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge and it is typically £8,000–£20,000. Gas, power and water connections are priced on top, and because the clay can affect base depth, the fixed written price follows our test hole.
Do I need planning permission for an outdoor kitchen in Oakham?
Usually not, if it sits in the rear garden. Cover is where care is needed: a roof or pergola must stay within the outbuilding height and size limits. Oakham's historic centre is a conservation area with the usual limits, but the town has no Article 4 direction. Planning here sits with Rutland County Council.
Will a kitchen on Oakham clay move or crack?
Clay that swells in winter and shrinks in a dry summer will move a kitchen built straight onto patio slabs. We build on a concrete base sized for the ground we find in the test hole, so the counters and worktops are not riding on the paving.
Where does the water from the sink go?
That is one of the first things we work out in Oakham, because the clay drains slowly and the town already has a surface water problem. We look at how the garden falls, what drainage is nearby and where the run-off can go, then design the kitchen around it.
