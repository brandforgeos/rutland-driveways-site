Driveways in Edith Weston

Driveways for every kind of Edith Weston house

Edith Weston sits on the south shore of Rutland Water, a stone village with a green and a number of substantial houses, next to St George's Barracks. Terraces, outdoor rooms and lighting designed around the views are the typical projects here.

Edith Weston specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Edith Weston

Edith Weston is on the limestone plateau above the reservoir, and most plots drain reasonably well, though clay pockets turn up. We test the ground before designing the base.

Edith Weston has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings, with natural materials the right choice in the conservation area.

Rutland Water itself carries negligible flood risk according to Rutland's flood strategy, and Edith Weston is not one of the places it names for river flooding.

Questions

Driveways in Edith Weston: questions

How much does a new driveway cost in Edith Weston?
For a typical Edith Weston drive of 40–75m², resin bound costs roughly £6,000–£16,500, block paving £4,750–£12,750 and tarmac £3,500–£9,750, all in.
How do I get a dropped kerb in Edith Weston?
You apply to Rutland County Council: £194 for a Section 184 vehicle access permit (one or two homes). It is built by an approved contractor with street works accreditation and the right insurance, working under the council's permit. See dropped kerbs in Edith Weston.
Do I need planning permission for a driveway in Edith Weston?
A permeable drive, or one that drains to your own lawn or border, needs no planning permission; more than 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road does.

Planning a driveway in Edith Weston?
