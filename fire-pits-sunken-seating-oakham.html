Fire pits & sunken seating in Oakham

Fire pits & sunken seating in Oakham

A fire pit gives an Oakham garden a reason to be used after the sun goes down, and a sunken seating area around it adds shelter and a sense of enclosure. We build both: pits set into the patio, lined and faced in stone or brick, and seating areas dug into the garden with retaining walls, built-in benches and steps. All prices on this page are guides. Once we have seen the garden and dug a test hole, the fixed price comes to you in writing.

Oakham gardens vary as much as its streets. Limestone and ironstone houses in the old town around the castle and the Market Place tend to have walled rear gardens where a compact pit suits best. Terraces and semis off Brooke Road, Braunston Road and Burley Road usually have long, narrow plots, and the estates at Barleythorpe and Oakham Heights often have open, level gardens with room for a full sunken area. We design to the plot, not to a catalogue.

Oakham specifically

What makes Oakham different

Clay is the whole story with a sunken seating area in Oakham. Dig into the Vale of Catmose and you have made a sump: rainwater runs off the lawn, collects at the lowest point and stays there because the clay will not take it. Oakham is also named in Rutland County Council's flood strategy as a surface water flood risk area of national significance. So the drain or soakaway at the bottom is sized from a test hole, and where a soakaway cannot work in clay we design another route for the water.

A typical Oakham job is a semi or terrace with a rear lawn that the family barely uses after September. We lift part of the lawn, dig a shallow sunken circle or square, build low retaining walls on footings with drainage behind, and face built-in benches in brick or stone to match the house. A lined fire pit sits in the centre, and a drain at the low point keeps the floor dry through the wet months.

You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Questions

Fire pits & sunken seating in Oakham: questions

How much is a fire pit in Oakham?
For an Oakham garden, a built-in stone or brick fire pit typically costs £1,200–£2,000, and a fire pit with built-in seating from about £3,500, as a guide. A sunken seating area with retaining walls and drainage is priced per job, usually as part of a wider garden build. On clay the drainage is a real part of the work, which is why we price after testing the ground.
Do I need planning permission in Oakham's conservation area?
Behind an Oakham house, a fire pit or sunken seating area in the rear garden does not normally need planning permission. The Oakham conservation area covers the historic centre, but there is no Article 4 direction in the town, so outside listed buildings the normal rules apply. Wherever you are, keep fires clear of fences, trees and buildings.
Can you build a sunken area on a Barleythorpe or Oakham Heights plot?
Yes. Newer estate gardens are often level and open, which suits a sunken area well. The ground underneath is still Vale clay, so the same rules apply: retaining walls on proper footings, drainage behind them, and a drain or soakaway at the bottom sized from a test hole on your plot.
My garden already holds water in winter. Is a sunken area a bad idea?
Not necessarily, but it changes the design. A garden that ponds tells us the clay is not taking water, so a simple soakaway may not be enough. We look at where the water can go before we dig, and if there is no sensible route we will tell you and suggest a level fire pit area instead.
