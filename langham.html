Langham specifically

Homes, ground and conservation in Langham

Langham is a large village just north west of Oakham, long known as the home of Ruddles brewery, with ironstone and limestone houses in the centre and later housing around it. Plots are often generous enough for sweeping drives and full rear gardens.

Langham sits on the edge of the Vale of Catmose clay, where water drains slowly, and its brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity. Base depth and drainage decide most designs here.

Langham has a conservation area with an Article 4 direction. Rutland's Article 4 directions can cover front walls, gates and fences, parking spaces, porches and roofs, so we check the Langham direction before designing anything facing the road.

Rutland's flood strategy notes that the Langham Brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity, and names Langham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk. Check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Rutland conservation areas and Article 4 list · Rutland flood risk strategy.

Council rules and fees for Langham

  • Dropped kerbs: £194 for a Section 184 vehicle access permit (one or two homes); built by an approved contractor with street works accreditation and the right insurance, working under the council's permit. Rutland County Council: dropped kerbs
  • Front driveways: A permeable drive, or one that drains to your own lawn or border, needs no planning permission; more than 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road does. Langham has an Article 4 direction, which can bring front walls, gates, parking spaces and porches under planning control, so we check it before designing anything that faces the road.
