Bin stores in Uppingham

Bin stores in Uppingham

Uppingham is a hilltop market town, and on plenty of its streets nothing is quite level. That matters more than you might expect with bins. A full wheelie bin on a slope wants to roll, a store on a slope racks out of square, and a path that tilts sideways makes collection day a balancing act. We fit composite slatted stores with aluminium doors for one, two or three bins, always on a level paved pad, and we plan the route to the kerb as part of the job.

Many properties in the centre have tight frontages or rear courtyards rather than front gardens, so space is the other thing we work around. We measure your bins, check that every lid opens fully inside the store and look at how the bins get from the store to the collection point, whether that is a few steps or a longer run from the back. A guide price is given on the visit, then a fixed price in writing.

Uppingham specifically

What makes Uppingham different

Uppingham stands on Northampton Sand ironstone, which usually drains better than the Vale clay around Oakham but varies within a single street. The bigger factor is the fall of the land. On a sloping plot we cut or build up the pad so the store sits dead level, and we give it enough fall to shed rain without turning it into a ramp. Because the slopes push water downhill, we also make sure run-off from the pad goes somewhere sensible rather than towards the house or a neighbour.

In the centre, a typical job is a slim one or two bin store tucked into a rear courtyard or a narrow frontage, set on a small paved pad. On the Victorian and interwar roads out of town, and on the later estates to the north and east, it is more often a two or three bin store at the side of the house with a level path to the pavement.

Bin stores in Uppingham: questions

What does a bin store cost in Uppingham?
As a guide, a single wheelie bin store fitted on a level paved base starts from about £1,000. Two and three bin stores, and any new path to the kerb, are priced on the visit. On a sloping Uppingham plot the amount of levelling affects the price, so we confirm a fixed figure in writing once we have seen it.
Is planning permission needed for a bin store in Uppingham?
Beside or behind the house, a bin store is normally permitted development. A free-standing store in front of the house technically falls outside householder permitted development, so we check with Rutland County Council first. The town centre is a conservation area. The town has no Article 4 direction, but villages such as Lyddington, Seaton, Ayston, Preston, Ridlington and Belton-in-Rutland do.
Our drive slopes down to the road. Can you still fit a bin store?
Yes. We set the store on its own level pad, cut into or built up from the slope, so it stands square and the doors close properly. Then we look at the route to the kerb, because wheeling a full bin down a steep drive needs a sensible line and a firm, even surface.
We only have a small courtyard. Is there room for a bin store?
Often there is. Single and two bin stores take up less space than people expect, and we measure your bins and the courtyard to make sure the lids open fully and you can still get past. If space is very tight, we will tell you honestly on the visit.
