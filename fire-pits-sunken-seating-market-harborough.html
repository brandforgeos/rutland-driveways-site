Fire pits & sunken seating in Market Harborough

Fire pits & sunken seating in Market Harborough

After the surface water flooding in the town centre in September 2024, and the floods of July 2013 before it, nobody in Market Harborough needs telling that water matters. That is the starting point for any sunken seating area we build here. The fire pit itself is the easy part: lined, set into the patio and faced in brick or stone. The retaining walls, benches, steps and above all the drain at the bottom are where the design work goes. Prices here are guides, fixed in writing after the visit.

Gardens across town differ. Victorian and Edwardian streets out towards Little Bowden and Great Bowden often have long, narrow rear gardens that suit a pit at the far end, away from the house. Later estates around the edge have more regular plots, and gardens at Airfield Farm near Lubenham are newer and often open, with room for a full sunken area once the ground has been checked. Each of those needs a different layout, and we draw it to suit.

Market Harborough specifically

What makes Market Harborough different

Jurassic mudstones and clays underlie Market Harborough, and they drain slowly. A sunken seating area dug into them collects water from the surrounding lawn and patio, and without a planned outlet it simply fills. A soakaway can work on some plots but not on others, so we test the ground before choosing one. Where it will not drain, we design another route for the water or recommend keeping the seating at ground level. Permeable paving around the pit is worth considering where it suits the ground.

A typical Market Harborough job is a Victorian or Edwardian house with a long rear garden, where the owners want a fire pit beyond the patio rather than next to the house. We build a shallow sunken area at the far end, with low retaining walls on footings, brick or stone benches on two sides, and a drain or soakaway at the low point sized from a test hole.

Fire pits & sunken seating in Market Harborough: questions

What does a fire pit cost in Market Harborough?
Guide prices in Market Harborough: a built-in stone or brick fire pit typically costs £1,200–£2,000, and a fire pit with built-in seating from about £3,500. A sunken seating area with retaining walls and drainage is priced per job. On slow-draining ground the drainage design affects the final figure, and we confirm it in writing after testing your garden.
Do I need planning permission for a fire pit in Market Harborough?
In Market Harborough, a fire pit or sunken seating area in a rear garden does not normally need planning permission. The town's conservation area covers the historic core, and an Article 4 direction on land at Queen's Street, Little Bowden, covers gates, fences and walls. If new seating walls there would face the road, check with Harborough District Council.
Will a sunken area make flooding worse in my garden?
The aim is that it does not, because the water that collects in it is drained away by design. Built badly, it becomes a pond. In Market Harborough we test how your ground drains, then size the drain or soakaway to suit, and we will tell you if a sunken design is the wrong choice for your plot.
We are at Airfield Farm. Is the ground ready for a fire pit area?
New gardens still sit on the same slow-draining mudstone and clay as the rest of town. We dig on the visit to see what is there, because retaining walls need proper footings and the drain at the bottom has to suit the ground it sits in, not the fresh turf on top.
