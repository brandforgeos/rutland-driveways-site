Glass verandas in Empingham

Glass verandas in Empingham

Empingham sits at the eastern end of Rutland Water, below the dam, and many gardens here look out over the water or open country. That changes what a veranda is for. It is less about squeezing cover into a small yard and more about a dry place to sit and look out. A glass roof stays clearer than polycarbonate, so it suits a garden where the view is the point, and the slim aluminium frame keeps the sight lines open.

Plots in the village are often generous, which opens up the 6m × 3m size for a dining table and a seating area under one roof. Side screens on one or both ends can make the space usable earlier and later in the year, and LED lighting and heaters can be built in to suit. Around Empingham we also cover Exton, Whitwell, Normanton, Edith Weston and Tickencote, and Oakham and Ketton are close by.

Empingham specifically

What makes Empingham different

Empingham is on the limestone side of Rutland, and much of the village drains reasonably well, which helps with a soakaway for the downpipe. The North Brook, a tributary of the Gwash, runs through the village, though, and ground near it stays wet; Rutland's flood strategy traces the brook through Cottesmore, Empingham and Ryhall. In the stone-built centre around the church and the main street, the conservation area applies, and natural materials are the safe choice for any paving under the veranda.

A typical Empingham veranda runs across the back of a stone house in the centre or a later house on the village edge, sometimes at the larger 6m × 3m size to suit the garden. It involves post footings, a wall plate and flashing, a glass roof chosen for the view, and a downpipe to a soakaway, with side screens and lighting added where wanted.

Questions

Glass verandas in Empingham: questions

How much should I budget for a veranda in Empingham?
The guide is £9,000–£14,000 supplied and fitted for a 4m × 3m aluminium and glass veranda, and £15,000–£22,000 for a 6m × 3m, which many larger Empingham gardens can take. Side screens, lighting and heaters are on top, and your fixed price arrives in writing after we visit.
Does Empingham's conservation area affect a veranda?
It means the rules are tighter than elsewhere, but Empingham has no Article 4 direction. A rear veranda is usually permitted development within the extension depth and height limits, away from a road and on a house that is not listed. Open-sided verandas under 30m² are usually exempt from building regulations. We check your address with you first.
Glass or polycarbonate if I want to keep the view?
Glass. It looks better, stays clearer and is quieter in rain. Polycarbonate is lighter and cheaper and still keeps you dry, but for a garden that looks over open country or the water, a clear glass roof is usually worth the difference.
My garden runs down towards the North Brook. Is that a problem?
Not for the veranda, which sits by the house, but it affects where the roof water goes. Ground near the brook stays wet, so a soakaway placed down the garden may not drain. We test nearer the house and choose a route the ground can actually take.
