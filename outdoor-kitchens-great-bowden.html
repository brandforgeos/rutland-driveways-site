Outdoor kitchens in Great Bowden

Outdoor kitchens in Great Bowden

Great Bowden is a village of greens, with ironstone, brick and thatched houses close together around them. That makes the position of a grill or pizza oven more important than in most places. We plan where heat, sparks and smoke go, keeping cooking well away from any thatched roof and away from neighbours' windows. Then we build on a concrete base, with block counters faced to suit the house and weatherproof worktops.

Prices on this page are guides, and a fixed price follows in writing after the visit, once we have looked at the roofs and windows around the garden, tested the clay and measured the service runs back to the house. Access to older houses near the greens can be tight, so we check that too. We cover Great Bowden and Market Harborough, Little Bowden, Welham, Slawston and Foxton, with Corby and Uppingham close by.

Great Bowden specifically

What makes Great Bowden different

The village sits on the clays and mudstones of the Welland valley, which drain slowly, with the River Welland and the Langton Brook to the south. Harborough's flood records note the April 1998 flood, when both overtopped and affected part of Great Bowden Lane. We design for the water first. Great Bowden has a conservation area covering the village and its greens, and a neighbourhood plan with design and character policies, so visible changes should respect the materials around them.

Behind an older ironstone or brick house near the greens, a typical outdoor kitchen is a modest stone or brick-faced run with a grill and worktop, positioned well away from the house roof. On the later housing at the village edges, gardens often allow a fuller L-shape with a sink, fridge and pizza oven beside a patio.

Outdoor kitchens in Great Bowden: questions

What does an outdoor kitchen cost in Great Bowden?
A Great Bowden kitchen with built-in counters, worktop and appliance space starts from about £6,000. With a grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge included, it is typically £8,000–£20,000. Gas, power and water connections are priced on top, and the fixed written figure follows our visit.
Does Great Bowden's conservation area or neighbourhood plan affect an outdoor kitchen?
Not normally. A kitchen in the rear garden falls outside planning in most cases, and a roof or pergola over it has to meet the outbuilding height and size limits. The conservation area and neighbourhood plan expect visible changes to respect local character. Harborough District Council handles planning.
We have a thatched roof. Can we still have a pizza oven?
Often, yes, but position is everything. We site the oven and grill well away from the thatch and think about the prevailing wind and where smoke and sparks will travel. If the garden cannot allow safe distance, we will say so.
How do you stop the kitchen area becoming a puddle?
The clay here drains slowly, so we set falls on the worktops and surrounding paving, and plan where the run-off goes, before the base is laid. A permeable surface around the kitchen can help.
