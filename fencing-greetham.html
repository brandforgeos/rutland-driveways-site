Fencing in Greetham

Fencing in Greetham

Greetham is a limestone village just west of the A1, with stone cottages and farm buildings at its core, later infill and estate housing around them, and Greetham Valley on its edge. Owners here often come to us when a drive is being widened, because widening it changes where the side boundary sits and the old fence rarely survives the move. We usually plan the new fence line alongside the drive so both are right.

We put up closeboard timber on concrete posts with gravel boards, and composite or aluminium slatted fencing on aluminium posts. For the older stone properties, a natural timber or warm-toned slatted finish sits comfortably against the limestone. For later houses, owners have more freedom, and a slatted screen with LED-lit capping can turn a plain side passage into something tidy. For low-lying plots, we set gravel boards and posts with extra care, because wetter ground shortens the life of any timber left in it.

Greetham specifically

What makes Greetham different

Greetham sits on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which generally drains well and holds a post firmly once it is set in concrete. Rutland's flood strategy names Greetham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, so low-lying plots get more thought, with gravel boards and post depth chosen for wetter ground. The village has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, so outside listed buildings normal permitted development applies.

A typical Greetham job follows a drive widening: the side fence is moved, rebuilt on new concrete posts with gravel boards and closeboard, and finished with a matching gate between the drive and the rear garden. On the older stone properties, the finish is usually natural timber; on later houses, owners often choose a slatted panel near the patio for a cleaner look.

Fencing in Greetham: questions

What does a fence cost in Greetham?
Expect £80–£100 per metre supplied and fitted on concrete posts for closeboard, and from about £240 per metre fitted at 1.8m high for composite or aluminium slats. The cost of removing the old fence is set on the visit, and if the job goes with a drive widening we price both together in writing.
Can I fence my front garden in Greetham?
Next to a road or footpath used by vehicles, a fence is normally permitted development up to 1m; elsewhere up to 2m. Greetham has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction. Listed buildings need consent, and some estates have front boundary conditions.
We are widening our drive. Can you move the fence at the same time?
Yes, and it is the sensible order to do it in. We set the new boundary line, rebuild the fence on new posts, and add a gate so the drive and garden work together.
Which fence finish suits a Greetham stone cottage?
Natural timber closeboard or warm, stone-coloured slatted panels sit best against limestone. Cool greys can look out of place next to old stone, though they may suit a later house on the edge of the village.
