Driveway gates in Oakham

Driveway gates in Oakham

Oakham has three quite different kinds of frontage, and each asks something different of a gate. Stone houses around the castle, All Saints and the Market Place often have walls and piers already. Victorian and interwar semis off Brooke Road, Braunston Road and Burley Road tend to have narrower drives where swing leaves need careful measuring. On the Barleythorpe and Oakham Heights estates, open-plan fronts are often being closed off for the first time, which means building new posts or piers from scratch.

We fit sliding and swing gates in aluminium and composite, matched to any fencing you have, and we automate them with keypads, intercoms or remote fobs as you prefer. Every automated gate gets safety edges or photocells and is force-tested to the standard once it is working. If you already have gates in good order, automating them is usually £1,500–£3,000, which is often the sensible route for Oakham owners with solid timber or metal gates that simply need a motor.

Oakham specifically

What makes Oakham different

Most of Oakham sits on the heavy clay of the Vale of Catmose, which swells when wet and shrinks in a dry summer. A gate post that moves even slightly will make leaves drop, catch or strain the motor, so we size and depth the footings for the clay rather than a standard hole. The town is also named in Rutland's flood strategy for surface water, so we keep control boxes and electrics clear of where water sits. In the conservation area around the centre, road-facing gates can need permission; elsewhere the usual permitted development limits apply.

A typical Oakham job is a semi or detached house on one of the newer Barleythorpe streets with an open-plan front, where the owner wants a pair of automated swing gates set back far enough to open inwards without blocking the car. It involves new posts on deep footings, a cable run from the house for the motors, a keypad or fobs, and photocells across the opening.

Questions

Driveway gates in Oakham: questions

How much do electric gates cost in Oakham?
Guide prices for Oakham: automated swing gates typically £4,000–£8,000 supplied and fitted, a new automated sliding gate £5,000–£10,000, and automating gates you already have usually £1,500–£3,000. Power is extra. Once we have measured the opening and dug a test hole in the clay, you receive a fixed written price.
Do I need planning permission for gates in Oakham?
In most of the town, no. Gates are normally permitted development up to 2m high, or 1m next to a road used by vehicles, and must not open outwards over the footway or road. Oakham has no Article 4 direction, but in the conservation area or on a listed house, gates facing the road can need permission from Rutland County Council.
Will clay ground make my gate posts move?
It can if the footings are shallow. Vale of Catmose clay swells in a wet winter and shrinks in a dry summer, and a post that shifts makes leaves sag or bind. We dig to find what is there and set the post foundations deep and wide enough for the clay and the gate's weight.
Our drive floods in heavy rain. Is that a problem for an electric gate?
It needs planning around. Oakham has a known surface water risk, so we place the control box and connections above where water collects, and on a sliding gate we think hard about the track. Sorting the drive's drainage at the same time is often worth doing.
