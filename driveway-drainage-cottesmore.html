Driveway drainage in Cottesmore

Driveway drainage in Cottesmore

Cottesmore sits on mixed ground on the edge of the limestone, and the North Brook rises nearby. That combination means one garden can take water freely while the next holds it for days. We do not design drainage on the strength of a neighbour's experience. On the visit we dig a test hole, look at the drive's falls and where water is gathering, and then decide between a soakaway, a crate system or a permeable area.

Family homes here often want a wider drive, and widening is exactly when drainage needs thinking about. Turning lawn into hard standing sends more water to the same low point, and an extension that ties into an older drive can trap water at the joint. Rutland Driveways & Landscaping plans the channel and outlet as part of any widening, or puts right an existing drive that has started to puddle since it was enlarged.

Cottesmore specifically

What makes Cottesmore different

The old centre of Cottesmore has ironstone and limestone cottages, with a good deal of later housing around it and Kendrew Barracks, the former RAF Cottesmore, just outside. The ground beneath varies from plot to plot, from free-draining limestone to clay, and that decides how big a soakaway needs to be, or whether one will work at all. The North Brook runs from Cottesmore towards Empingham and Ryhall. Cottesmore has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, and drainage on your own drive needs no planning permission.

A typical Cottesmore job is a later family house whose drive has been widened over part of the front lawn and now pools where old meets new. We cut in a heavy-duty channel across the low point, take the water to a soakaway or crate system sized from a percolation test, and level the joint so the falls run one way.

Questions

Driveway drainage in Cottesmore: questions

How much does driveway drainage cost in Cottesmore?
In Cottesmore a channel drain across a typical driveway or garage entrance is about £1,000–£1,800 installed, or £120–£200 per metre for a heavy-duty, vehicle-rated channel. With ground this mixed, soakaways are sized and priced after a percolation test on your plot. We put a fixed price in writing after the visit.
Does drainage work in Cottesmore need planning permission?
Not for the drainage itself: work on your own driveway does not need planning permission. Cottesmore has a conservation area but no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings. Surface water from the drive must not be sent to the foul sewer or out onto the road.
We are widening our drive. Do we need extra drainage?
Usually yes. A wider hard surface collects more rain and sends it to the same low point, and lawn that used to soak some up is gone. We size the channel and soakaway for the finished drive, so the widening does not create a new puddle.
Our neighbour has a soakaway that works. Will one work for us?
Not necessarily. Cottesmore ground can change from limestone to clay between plots, so a soakaway that empties quickly next door can stay full on your side. A percolation test on your own plot is the only reliable guide.
