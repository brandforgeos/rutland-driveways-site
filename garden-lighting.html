Questions

Things people ask us

How much does garden lighting cost?
Garden lighting is cheapest when the ducting goes in as the patio, steps and walls are built; adding it later means lifting paving. We design it as a scheme of a few well-placed fittings, with the electrical work done and certified by a registered electrician.
Can you add lighting to an existing patio?
Yes, around the edges, in walls and in beds. Lights set into the paving itself usually mean lifting slabs.
