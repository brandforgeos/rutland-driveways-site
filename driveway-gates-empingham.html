Driveway gates in Empingham

Driveway gates in Empingham

Empingham sits at the eastern end of Rutland Water, below the dam, and many plots here are generous, with the water or open country beyond. On a long drive, the gate is often well away from the house, which affects two things: how far the power has to travel, and how you open it for visitors. An intercom or keypad at the gate, with remote fobs for the family, is the usual answer, and the power supply is priced separately once we know the distance.

The stone-built centre around the church and main street is a conservation area, where natural materials and a restrained design are the safe choice. On the later houses at the edges of the village, there is more freedom. We fit sliding and swing gates in aluminium and composite, matched to existing fencing where there is some, and force-test every automated gate to the standard. Where the drive slopes towards the lane, we look at sliding options first.

Empingham specifically

What makes Empingham different

Empingham is on the limestone side of Rutland, and much of the village drains well, which gives gate footings firm, reliable ground. The North Brook is the exception: it runs through the village on its way to the Gwash, and Rutland's flood strategy traces its course here. Ground near the brook stays wet, so posts need deeper footings and motors should not sit in the low spot. The conservation area covers the centre, but there is no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development applies outside listed buildings.

A typical Empingham job is a detached house on the edge of the village with a long drive and open country behind. The owners want an automated gate set back from the lane, with an intercom or keypad for deliveries. It usually involves new posts on concrete footings, a sliding or swing gate, a longer cable run from the house, and photocells and safety edges.

Questions

Driveway gates in Empingham: questions

What does an automated gate cost in Empingham?
On Empingham's larger plots, a new automated sliding gate typically costs £5,000–£10,000 supplied and fitted, and automated swing gates £4,000–£8,000. Automating existing gates is usually £1,500–£3,000. The power supply comes on top and can be a bigger item on a long drive, so we measure the run and put a fixed price in writing after the visit.
Do I need planning permission for gates in Empingham?
Not usually. Empingham has no Article 4 direction, so gates are normally permitted development up to 2m high, or 1m next to a road used by vehicles, as long as they do not open outwards over the footway or road. Within the conservation area or on a listed house, road-facing gates can need permission from Rutland County Council.
Our gate will be a long way from the house. How do visitors get in?
An intercom or keypad at the gate is the usual answer, so callers can be let in from the house and regular visitors can use a code. Family members carry remote fobs. We plan the cable route for power and controls on the visit and include it in the price.
Is ground near the North Brook a problem for gate posts?
It needs more care. Ground near the brook stays wet, which can let a shallow footing move. We dig to see what is there and design deeper footings where needed, and keep motors and the control box out of the lowest part of the entrance.
