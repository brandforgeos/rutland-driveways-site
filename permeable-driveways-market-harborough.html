Permeable driveways in Market Harborough

Permeable driveways in Market Harborough

In September 2024 Market Harborough town centre flooded from surface water when a high River Welland stopped the drains discharging, and in July 2013 more than 50 properties flooded. With that history, a driveway that sends every drop to the road is part of the problem. A permeable driveway lets rain soak through the surface and the base instead. We build them in resin bound, permeable block paving or stabilised gravel, each on an open-graded base designed for what is underneath.

What is underneath here is usually Jurassic mudstone and clay, which drains slowly. Where the ground will not take the water, we line the permeable base and release it through a controlled outlet, holding it back rather than letting it rush off. Which route suits your drive depends on a test of the ground, which we carry out on the visit. The fixed written price follows, and it reflects whether a lined system is needed.

Market Harborough specifically

What makes Market Harborough different

The town sits where the River Welland meets the River Jordan, and when the Welland runs high the drains cannot discharge. The Victorian and Edwardian streets out to Little Bowden and Great Bowden have older frontages, while estates around the edge, including the large Airfield Farm development near Lubenham, have newer drives. The conservation area, designated in 1969, covers the historic core around the Square and the Old Grammar School. An Article 4 direction served in 2020 at Queen's Street, Little Bowden, covers gates, fences and walls.

A common job here is an Edwardian or Victorian house towards Little Bowden where an old impermeable frontage drains straight to the road. We break it out, test how the clay drains, and build a lined or unlined open-graded base depending on the result, then lay permeable block paving or resin bound. On the newer estates, it is often widening a drive for a second car.

Permeable driveways in Market Harborough: questions

What does a permeable drive cost in Market Harborough?
On guide prices, permeable resin bound is £150–£220 per m², permeable block paving £140–£210 per m² and stabilised gravel £60–£100 per m², all in. Much of Market Harborough is slow-draining clay, where a lined system with a controlled outlet adds to that, so the ground test shapes the final written price.
Do I need planning permission for a permeable drive in Market Harborough?
A permeable new or replacement front driveway needs no planning permission, and nor does one draining to a lawn or border on your own land. The 2020 Article 4 direction at Queen's Street, Little Bowden, covers gates, fences and walls, so boundary changes there need checking with Harborough District Council.
Can a permeable drive help with the town's surface water problems?
It keeps rain from your drive out of the road drains, which are what backed up in September 2024 when the Welland was high. It cannot promise to keep a property dry, but it does stop your drive adding to the water sent to those drains all at once.
We're on Airfield Farm. Why would the ground matter on a new house?
Market Harborough sits on Jurassic mudstones and clays, and a new house does not change what is under the garden. We test the ground on newer estates such as Airfield Farm near Lubenham just as we do in the older streets, and if it drains slowly, a lined system with a controlled outlet handles the water instead.
