Outdoor kitchens in Greetham

Outdoor kitchens in Greetham

Greetham's historic core of stone cottages and farm buildings sets the tone for its gardens, and an outdoor kitchen faced in stone colours fits in far better than a bright modern finish. We build on a concrete base, raise block counters, face them to suit the house, and fit weatherproof worktops. The grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge are planned in from the start, along with the route for each service.

The prices on this page are guides. A fixed price follows in writing after the visit, once the access, the ground and the distance to the house are known, and once we have checked whether the plot sits on lower ground. We cover Greetham village, the estate housing and infill around it and Greetham Valley, plus Cottesmore, Stretton, Clipsham, Exton and Thistleton, with Oakham and Empingham also within easy reach.

Greetham specifically

What makes Greetham different

Greetham sits on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which generally drains well and gives a sound footing for a kitchen base. The exception is lower ground: Rutland's flood strategy names Greetham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, so on those plots we look harder at base height and water routes. The village has a conservation area with no Article 4 direction, and stone-coloured materials suit it best, both at the front and in the garden.

Behind a converted farm building or stone cottage, a typical kitchen is a stone-faced run against an old wall with a pizza oven as the centrepiece. On later infill and estate homes, it is more often an L-shape with a grill, sink and fridge beside a new or replaced patio.

Outdoor kitchens in Greetham: questions

What does an outdoor kitchen cost in Greetham?
In Greetham a built-in kitchen with cabinets, worktop and appliance space starts from about £6,000, while one fully equipped with grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge is typically £8,000–£20,000. Gas, power and water connections are priced on top once we know the distances, and the fixed figure follows in writing.
Do I need planning for an outdoor kitchen in Greetham?
A rear garden kitchen does not normally need planning. The outbuilding height and size limits apply if you add a roof or pergola over it. Greetham has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction; any application would go to Rutland County Council.
Can a pizza oven be built against an old stone wall?
It can sit alongside one, but we keep the oven on its own base and allow the clearances it needs, rather than loading or heating an old wall that was never built for it.
Does good drainage mean I can skip a proper base?
Not at all. Free-draining limestone helps with water, but a heavy kitchen still needs a concrete base of its own. Building on patio slabs is the commonest reason outdoor kitchens crack or sink.
