Driveways in Ryhall

Driveways for every kind of Ryhall house

Ryhall, with Belmesthorpe, sits on the River Gwash just north of Stamford, with stone cottages around the green and the church, and later estate housing. Being so close to Stamford, many homes here were built by the same post-war and modern housebuilders.

Ryhall specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Ryhall

Ryhall is on the limestone, which generally drains well, but the Gwash runs through the village and ground near the river is wetter. We check each site before choosing between a soakaway and channel drainage.

Ryhall has a conservation area covering the historic village and no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings.

The River Gwash runs through Ryhall, and Rutland's flood strategy names Ryhall among the places with a few properties at river flood risk.

Driveways in Ryhall: questions

How much does a new driveway cost in Ryhall?
For a typical Ryhall drive of 35–65m², resin bound costs roughly £5,250–£14,250, block paving £4,250–£11,000 and tarmac £3,250–£8,500, all in.
How do I get a dropped kerb in Ryhall?
You apply to Rutland County Council: £194 for a Section 184 vehicle access permit (one or two homes). It is built by an approved contractor with street works accreditation and the right insurance, working under the council's permit. See dropped kerbs in Ryhall.
Do I need planning permission for a driveway in Ryhall?
A permeable drive, or one that drains to your own lawn or border, needs no planning permission; more than 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road does.
