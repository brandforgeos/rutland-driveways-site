Outdoor kitchens in Stamford

Stamford has more than 600 listed buildings in local limestone, and an outdoor kitchen here should look as if it belongs. We build the carcass in block on its own concrete base, then face it in stone or render in tones that suit the house, and top it with a weatherproof worktop. The grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge are part of the plan from day one, along with how the gas, power and water reach them.

Every figure on this page is a guide. After the visit we send a fixed price in writing, based on what we found: whether materials come through the house or a side gate, the distance for services, and whether there are cellars, rubble or flood plain ground under the garden. We cover the centre, the Victorian roads and the newer estates on the north side, and nearby villages including Tinwell, Great Casterton, Little Casterton, Barnack, Uffington, Collyweston and Easton on the Hill.

Stamford specifically

What makes Stamford different

Stamford was Britain's first conservation area, and since December 2018 an Article 4 direction across it removes permitted development for hard surfaces, walls, gates, fences, porches and extensions that face a road, footpath or open space. A kitchen tucked behind the house is normally outside that, but corner plots and gardens beside a footpath need checking. The town sits on Lincolnshire Limestone, which usually drains well, though old cellars, rubble fill and the Welland flood plain change things locally.

Behind a Victorian house on Empingham Road or Tinwell Road, a typical layout is a stone-faced run along the garden wall with a grill and a worktop, sometimes with a pergola over it. In the 1920s houses around Melbourne Road, gardens tend to be wider and an L-shape with a sink is common. On the new estates to the north, the kitchen often goes beside a new patio.

  1. PlanningAn outdoor kitchen in a rear garden does not normally need planning permission. A roof or pergola over it has to meet the outbuilding height and size limits. Gas work must be done by a Gas Safe engineer, and new outdoor circuits are notifiable electrical work. Stamford's Article 4 direction brings some work facing a road, footpath or open space under planning control, so we check it against your address first. Planning Portal: outbuildings

Outdoor kitchens in Stamford: questions

What is the price of an outdoor kitchen in Stamford?
A built-in Stamford kitchen with cabinets, worktop and appliance space starts from about £6,000. Equipped with grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge, the guide is typically £8,000–£20,000. Gas, power and water connections are priced on top, and natural stone facing to suit an older house is reflected in the fixed written figure.
Does Stamford's Article 4 direction affect an outdoor kitchen?
It removes permitted development for things like walls, fences, hard surfaces and extensions that face a road, footpath or open space in the conservation area. A rear garden kitchen does not normally need planning, but if yours would face one of those, or have a roof meeting the outbuilding limits, we check with South Kesteven District Council first.
Can the kitchen be faced to match an old stone house?
Yes, in stone or render. The council's appraisal favours natural materials in the conservation area, and the same thinking works in the garden. We face the counters in stone or render in colours that sit with the house rather than bright grey.
We live near the Welland. Does that matter?
The Environment Agency runs a flood warning area for properties near the River Welland in Stamford. If your garden is in it, we think about the height of the base and the position of the fridge and electrics before agreeing a layout.
