Garage conversions in Kettering

What garage conversions in Kettering usually involve

If your garage is really just somewhere the clutter lives, converting it is the cheapest way to gain a proper room — an office, snug, utility, playroom or extra bedroom — without extending the footprint. We convert integral, attached and detached garages across Kettering and the Northamptonshire villages, bringing the floor, walls and roof up to habitable standard so the space is warm and dry all year. Most conversions here are permitted development, and we confirm that for your property before starting.

What a proper Kettering garage conversion involves

A genuine conversion is far more than boarding over the garage door — that is what meets Building Regulations and stops the room feeling cold and damp.

The floor is insulated, given a damp proof membrane tied into the house and raised to match the adjoining rooms, so there is no step down onto a cold slab.

The walls, including the old garage opening, are rebuilt or lined, insulated and plastered, with a window or door to suit the property. We match the new frontage to the existing brick so it never looks like a filled-in garage.

Roof, heating, electrics and ventilation are all brought up to standard. Many Kettering homes, particularly on the newer estates, have a single integral garage that converts neatly into a downstairs office or snug off the hall.

Permitted development or planning?

Most garage conversions are permitted development because the work is internal and the footprint does not change. The usual exceptions are listed buildings, conservation-area properties, and newer estate homes where permitted-development rights were removed by a planning condition. We check the planning history for your address first and tell you where you stand.

Ground, drainage and conservation in Kettering

Ground around Kettering ranges from ironstone to clay, and new-build plots are often compacted subsoil under a thin layer of topsoil. We check what is under each garden before choosing the base and the drainage.

Kettering has 26 conservation areas, including the town centre, Barton Seagrave, Burton Latimer, Geddington and Rothwell. Its Article 4 directions are site-specific, mostly older ones covering individual streets or buildings, so we check your address before designing anything that faces the road.

The River Ise runs along the east of the town, with river level stations at Barford Bridge and Slade Brook. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Garage conversions in Kettering: questions

Will I lose parking?
Most people converting a garage already park on the drive. If you want to keep off-road parking we can widen or improve the driveway as part of the project.
Is Building Control sign-off included?
Yes. Even where planning is not required, the conversion is built to Building Regulations and inspected by Building Control, with certification on completion.
How long does it take?
A typical single-garage conversion takes around three to five weeks depending on finishes and services.
Can the new room be a bedroom?
It can, where it meets the relevant Building Regulations including ventilation, heating and means of escape. We advise at the survey.
How much does a garage conversion cost in Kettering?
A full single garage conversion in Kettering typically costs £14,000–£19,000, and a part conversion to a utility room or store starts from about £9,500, with the building control fee paid to the council. See our price guides. Your fixed price comes in writing after the site visit.
Do I need planning permission for a garage conversion in Kettering?
Most garage conversions are internal and need no planning permission, unless a planning condition keeps the garage for parking or the house is listed. Building regulations approval is always needed.
Who handles building control in Kettering?
North Northamptonshire Council building control. The fee is quoted by the council for each job (no published schedule). We make the application and arrange the inspections as part of the job.
