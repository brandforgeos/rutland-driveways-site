Bin stores in Kettering

Bin stores in Kettering

Close to Kettering town centre and along the Headlands, many Victorian and Edwardian homes have small front gardens and no side access, so the bins live at the front whether you like it or not. A well-built store there makes a real difference to how the house looks from the street. We fit composite slatted stores with aluminium doors for one, two or three bins, on a level paved pad with a clear route to the pavement.

Further out, on the interwar and post-war estates, at Barton Seagrave and on the new Hanwood Park development, there is usually more room and the store can go at the side. Wherever it goes, we measure your bins, make sure the lids open fully inside and look at the ground underneath. You get a guide price on the visit and a fixed price in writing once the position and pad are agreed.

Kettering specifically

What makes Kettering different

Ground around Kettering ranges from ironstone to clay, and new-build plots are often compacted subsoil under a thin layer of topsoil. A paving pad needs a proper sub-base on either, so we check what is under the spot before choosing the build-up. Planning is the other local wrinkle. With 26 conservation areas, including the town centre and Barton Seagrave, and Article 4 directions that mostly cover individual streets or buildings, the rules can differ between neighbouring roads, so we check your exact address first.

On a Victorian terrace near the centre, a typical job is a one or two bin store in the front garden on a small paved pad, opening straight onto the pavement. On a Hanwood Park or Barton Seagrave home, it is more often a two or three bin store at the side of the house, with a paved path out to the front.

Sources: North Northamptonshire Article 4 directions · Hanwood Park.

Questions

Bin stores in Kettering: questions

What is the cost of a bin store in Kettering?
As a guide, a single wheelie bin store fitted on a level paved base starts from about £1,000. Two and three bin stores, and any new path to the kerb, are priced on the visit, as they depend on the size of the pad and the route. The fixed figure comes in writing after we have measured up.
Do I need permission for a front garden bin store in Kettering?
A free-standing store in front of the house technically falls outside householder permitted development, so we check with North Northamptonshire Council before building one. Kettering's Article 4 directions are site-specific and cover individual streets or buildings, and there are 26 conservation areas, so we look up your address. Side and rear positions are normally permitted development.
My terrace has no side access. Will a front store look cluttered?
A store usually looks tidier than three loose bins. We size it to your bins and the garden, keep the pad neat and square to the house, and make sure the doors and lids open without blocking the path, so the front stays usable and the bins are out of sight.
Is a new-build garden at Hanwood Park firm enough for a pad?
Often it is compacted subsoil under a thin layer of topsoil, which can look firm but is not a base in itself. We strip the topsoil, check what is underneath and build a proper sub-base, so the pad stays level once the store is full.
