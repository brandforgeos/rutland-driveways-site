Driveway drainage in Uppingham

Driveway drainage in Uppingham

Uppingham is a hilltop town, and on sloping streets rain does not just fall on a drive, it travels across it. Drives that fall towards a garage or a front door collect the water from the whole surface and deliver it to the lowest, worst place. We fix that by setting a heavy-duty channel across the slope at the right point, taking the water somewhere it can safely go, and correcting falls where the drive has settled. Uppingham is not named in Rutland's flood strategy for river flooding, but surface water still needs a planned route.

The houses vary as much as the ground. In the centre around the school, the High Street and the Market Place, many ironstone and limestone properties have tight frontages or rear courtyards rather than front gardens, which leaves little room for a soakaway. Victorian and interwar houses on the roads out usually have more space but older, tired drives. The later estates to the north and east tend to have wider frontages where a channel, a soakaway and corrected falls can all fit comfortably.

Uppingham specifically

What makes Uppingham different

Uppingham stands on the Northampton Sand ironstone, which usually drains better than the clay around Oakham, but it varies within a single street, so we never assume. A percolation test tells us whether a soakaway will work on your plot. The bigger issue is the gradient: water gathers speed down a sloping drive, so channels need to be heavy-duty, properly haunched and placed to catch the flow. Older drains in the town can also be part of the problem, and we look at where the existing water is going before designing anything new.

A typical Uppingham job is a house on one of the roads out of town with a drive that drops towards an integral garage. We fit a vehicle-rated channel across the garage mouth or the top of the slope, connect it to a soakaway sized from a percolation test, and adjust the falls so water no longer runs under the garage door.

Questions

Driveway drainage in Uppingham: questions

What does driveway drainage cost in Uppingham?
A channel drain across a typical driveway or garage entrance in Uppingham costs about £1,000–£1,800 installed, or £120–£200 per metre for a heavy-duty, vehicle-rated channel, and steeper drives sometimes need the heavier option. A soakaway is sized and priced after a percolation test. You get a fixed price in writing once we have seen the slope and the ground.
Do I need permission for drainage work in or around Uppingham?
In Uppingham itself, drainage on your own driveway needs no planning permission and there is no Article 4 direction. Nearby villages including Lyddington, Seaton, Ayston, Preston, Ridlington and Belton-in-Rutland do have one, and there parking spaces can need permission if the drive surface is being redone. Drive water must stay out of the foul sewer and off the road.
Our drive slopes down to the garage. How do we stop water getting in?
The answer is usually a heavy-duty channel across the full width just in front of the garage door, with the water piped to a soakaway or permeable area, plus a check that the drive is not falling into the garage more steeply than it should. Where the base has dipped, we correct the falls too.
We only have a small courtyard in the town centre. Is there anywhere for the water to go?
Often there is, but it takes more thought. On a tight Uppingham courtyard we look at short channel runs, compact crate systems where the ground allows, and whether an existing surface water connection can be used. Surface water cannot go into the foul sewer, so we check what is there first.
