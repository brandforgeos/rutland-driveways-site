Fire pits & sunken seating in Greetham

Free-draining ground makes a sunken seating area simpler, and much of Greetham sits on it. That does not mean drainage can be skipped, only that a soakaway at the low point usually does the job. We build lined fire pits set into the patio, and sunken seating with retaining walls on footings, built-in benches, steps and drainage. All the prices shown are guide prices, and a fixed price is put in writing once we have visited and seen the ground for ourselves.

Greetham, just west of the A1, has a historic core of stone cottages and farm buildings, later infill and estate housing, and Greetham Valley on its edge. Older properties often have stone walls around the garden that a seating area can sit alongside, while newer houses tend to have open, regular plots that suit a sunken square or circle. We also work in Cottesmore, Stretton, Clipsham, Exton and Thistleton. Each layout starts from the ground we find.

Greetham specifically

What makes Greetham different

Greetham sits on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which generally drains well, so a sunken area with a soakaway beneath its floor is usually straightforward here. Rutland's flood strategy does name Greetham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, so low-lying plots get a closer look before we commit to a design. In the conservation area, stone-coloured materials suit the village, and we face walls and benches to sit with the cottages around them.

A typical Greetham job is an older stone property or a later house where the owners want an evening spot at the end of the garden. We dig a sunken square, build retaining walls faced in a stone-coloured material, run benches along three sides, add steps from the lawn and set a lined fire pit at the centre over a soakaway.

Fire pits & sunken seating in Greetham: questions

How much is a fire pit with seating in Greetham?
For Greetham gardens, a fire pit with built-in seating costs from about £3,500, and a built-in stone or brick fire pit typically £1,200–£2,000. A sunken seating area with retaining walls and drainage is priced per job, usually as part of a wider garden build here. We confirm your price in writing after the visit.
Do I need planning permission for a fire pit in Greetham?
Greetham rear gardens follow the general rule: a fire pit or sunken seating area there does not normally need planning permission. Greetham has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, so outside listed buildings normal permitted development applies. Keep fires a safe distance from fences, trees and buildings, and be considerate of neighbours.
If the ground drains well, why do I need a drain at all?
Because a sunken area collects water from everything around it, not just the rain that falls into it. Limestone helps, and in Greetham a soakaway is often enough, but it still has to be built and sized for the ground. Without one, even free-draining ground can leave puddles on the floor after heavy rain.
Can the seating sit against our old stone garden wall?
Often, as long as the wall is sound and we are not loading it with soil it was never built to hold back. Where a retaining wall is needed, we build a new one on proper footings with drainage behind, and face it to sit alongside the old stone.
