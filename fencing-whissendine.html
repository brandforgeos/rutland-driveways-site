Fencing in Whissendine

Fencing in Whissendine

Whissendine has a long main street of stone and brick houses, farm conversions and newer homes, and gardens here are often bigger than in town. Long boundaries mean the cost per metre matters, and closeboard timber on concrete posts with gravel boards is the sensible choice for most of the run. Where owners want a sharper finish near the house, composite or aluminium slatted fencing on aluminium posts never needs painting.

Farm conversions bring their own boundaries, often bordering paddocks or open land, where a neat fence marks the domestic garden from what lies beyond. We also fit matching gates and privacy screens, so a long boundary can include a gate through to a field or a screened corner for bins and storage. As the village is not a conservation area, owners have a wide choice of finish, though natural timber still suits the older stone and brick houses on Main Street.

Whissendine specifically

What makes Whissendine different

Whissendine is on the clay of the Vale, and the Whissendine Brook runs through the village. Ground drains slowly, so timber in contact with it rots and shallow posts lean. We set concrete or aluminium posts in concrete at the right depth and fit gravel boards under every closeboard panel. Rutland's flood strategy records that Main Street floods and becomes impassable, so on low plots we look at the water route first. Whissendine is not a conservation area and has no Article 4 direction.

A typical Whissendine job is a long rear garden behind a house on or near Main Street, where the old fence has rotted at the base. We replace the full run with concrete posts, gravel boards and closeboard, often adding a field gate at the bottom. The bottom gate is hung on posts set to take its weight.

Sources: Rutland flood risk strategy.

Questions

Fencing in Whissendine: questions

What will a long fence cost in Whissendine?
Closeboard at £80–£100 per metre supplied and fitted on concrete posts keeps long runs sensible here. Composite and aluminium slatted fencing is from about £240 per metre fitted at 1.8m high, so it is often kept to the area nearest the house. Old fencing is priced for removal at the visit, then we give a fixed written price.
Is planning permission needed for a Whissendine fence?
Whissendine is not a conservation area and has no Article 4 direction, so a fence is normally permitted development up to 2m, or 1m next to a road or footpath used by vehicles. Listed buildings still need consent.
Our old fence has rotted at the bottom. Why?
In slow-draining clay, panels and posts sitting in the soil stay damp for long periods. A new fence with gravel boards and concrete posts keeps the timber clear of the ground.
Can you fit a gate through to our paddock?
Yes. We build gates to match the fence and hang them on posts set to take the weight, at whatever width the gateway needs. If you need a wider gateway for machinery or a trailer, we size the gate and posts to suit rather than forcing a standard width.
