Block paving driveways in Empingham

What block paving driveways in Empingham usually involve

A block paved driveway is concrete or clay blocks laid on a screeded sand course over a compacted sub-base, with edges restrained and joints sanded. Laid properly it carries cars for decades, and individual blocks can be lifted for repairs.

Empingham sits at the eastern end of Rutland Water, below the dam, with a stone-built centre around the church and the main street, and later houses on its edges. Plots are often generous, and garden projects here frequently have the water or open country as a backdrop.

We cover Empingham (LE15) and the villages around it, including Exton, Whitwell, Normanton, Edith Weston and Tickencote.

Empingham specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Empingham

Empingham is on the limestone side of Rutland, and much of the village drains reasonably well, but the North Brook runs through it on its way to the Gwash, and ground near the brook stays wet.

Empingham has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction. Front-garden work outside listed buildings follows normal permitted development, with natural materials the safe choice in the conservation area.

The North Brook, a tributary of the Gwash, runs through Empingham, and Rutland's flood strategy traces it through Cottesmore, Empingham and Ryhall. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Rutland conservation areas · Rutland flood risk strategy.
