Driveway drainage in Ketton

Driveway drainage in Ketton

Ketton holds a particular place in Rutland's flood picture. The Welland flood warning area at Ketton is the only Environment Agency flood warning area in the county, and the flood strategy names the village among the places with a few properties at river flood risk. Most of Ketton sits on Lincolnshire Limestone, which takes a soakaway well, but the River Chater runs through the village to meet the Welland, and plots near it need a different approach. We find out which kind of ground your drive is on before designing anything.

The village's older streets are built of Ketton stone, the cream oolitic limestone quarried beside it, many under Collyweston slate. Drives and frontages here are often short and set against stone walls, and a drainage fix should not look out of place against them. Around the historic core, later 20th-century houses and bungalows have wider drives, typically concrete, block or tarmac, where puddling usually comes from settled bases and falls that have drifted towards the garage or front door.

Ketton specifically

What makes Ketton different

On the limestone, the usual Ketton fix is straightforward: a heavy-duty channel at the low point and a soakaway sized from a percolation test, often modest because the ground drains freely. Close to the Chater, the test can tell a very different story, and a permeable area or another route may be the better answer. Ketton has a conservation area with no Article 4 direction, and drainage on your own drive needs no planning permission. On Ketton stone frontages, buff and natural tones suit far better than grey.

A typical Ketton job is a later 20th-century bungalow or house with a drive that holds water by the garage. We cut in a vehicle-rated channel, run it to a soakaway sized from a percolation test in the limestone, and adjust the falls. On an older stone frontage, the channel and any repairs are finished in tones that sit with Ketton stone.

Questions

Driveway drainage in Ketton: questions

How much does it cost to fix a puddling drive in Ketton?
Around £1,000–£1,800 installed covers a channel drain across a typical Ketton driveway or garage entrance, or £120–£200 per metre for a heavy-duty, vehicle-rated channel. Soakaways are sized and priced after a percolation test, which on Ketton limestone often keeps them modest. The fixed price comes in writing after the visit.
Does Ketton's conservation area mean I need permission for drainage?
No. Drainage work on your own Ketton driveway does not need planning permission, and the village has no Article 4 direction despite its conservation area. Surface water still cannot go into the foul sewer or onto the road, and a new connection to a public sewer needs the water company's approval. Rutland County Council handles planning.
Is our garden too close to the River Chater for a soakaway?
It depends on the plot, which is why we test rather than guess. Ground close to the Chater can stay wet and drain far more slowly than the limestone further from the river. If the percolation test shows a soakaway will not cope, we look at a permeable area or another safe route.
Will a drainage channel look wrong against our Ketton stone house?
It should not. We choose a channel and grating that sit neatly in the drive, and keep any patching or edging in buff and natural tones that suit Ketton stone. The aim is a drive that drains properly without drawing attention to the fix.
