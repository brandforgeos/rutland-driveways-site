Outdoor kitchens in Edith Weston

Outdoor kitchens in Edith Weston

On the south shore of Rutland Water, Edith Weston has a number of substantial houses whose owners want to use the garden into the evening. Terraces, outdoor rooms and lighting designed around the views are the typical projects here, and a kitchen fits naturally into that. We build it on its own concrete base, with block or stone-faced counters and weatherproof worktops, and plan the grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge alongside seating and cover.

Figures on this page are guides; the fixed price follows in writing after the visit, once we have tested the ground, agreed how the kitchen sits within the terrace and measured the service runs back to the house. Planning the lighting at the same stage saves digging the garden up twice. We cover Edith Weston and nearby North Luffenham, South Luffenham, Lyndon, Manton and Normanton, as well as Empingham, Oakham and Uppingham.

Edith Weston specifically

What makes Edith Weston different

The village is on the limestone plateau above the reservoir, and most plots drain reasonably well, though clay pockets turn up. A kitchen that spans a clay pocket and firm limestone can settle unevenly, so we test the ground before designing the base. Rutland Water carries negligible flood risk according to Rutland's flood strategy. Edith Weston has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction; natural materials are the right choice in it.

Behind one of the larger stone houses, a typical outdoor kitchen is a stone-faced L or island set at the edge of a new terrace, with a grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge, under a pergola with lighting built in. The cook faces the seating and the view, and the counters give a little shelter from the wind off the water.

Questions

Outdoor kitchens in Edith Weston: questions

What does an outdoor kitchen cost in Edith Weston?
An Edith Weston outdoor kitchen with built-in cabinets, worktop and appliance space starts from about £6,000. A fully equipped version with grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge is typically £8,000–£20,000. Gas, power and water connections are priced on top, and a wider terrace or pergola is quoted alongside it in the fixed written price.
What planning rules apply in Edith Weston?
Not normally, for a kitchen in the rear garden. A roof or pergola above it is the part to watch, as it must meet the outbuilding height and size limits. The village has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, and Rutland County Council covers planning here.
Can the kitchen, terrace and lighting be done as one project?
Yes, and it usually works out better. Planning them together means the base, the power for lighting and the kitchen's services are laid once, and the layout is designed around how you will use the space.
Is an island layout possible?
On a large enough terrace, yes. An island lets the cook face the guests and the view, but it needs the gas, power and water brought up through the base, so we plan those routes before the concrete goes in.
