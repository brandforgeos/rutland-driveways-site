Built for stone-country homes

Built for stone-country homes

Rutland and the Welland valley are not like everywhere else. Driveways here sit in front of limestone cottages, Collyweston-roofed farmhouses and Georgian townhouses in England's first conservation area. Colours, edgings and drainage all have to suit.

Because we work here every week, we know which resin blends sit comfortably against Clipsham and Ketton stone, when a conservation area or listed setting needs a more careful approach, and how to handle the clay around Oakham and Market Harborough.

  • Permeable, SuDS-friendly surfacing as standard, so planning permission is usually not needed outside conservation areas such as Stamford's
  • Colour and material samples brought to your home, judged against your stone in daylight
  • Drainage designed for the job: channels, soakaways and falls that work

How it works

From first call to handover

Every project starts with a visit, not a price over the phone. We look at the ground, the falls, the access and where the water goes, because those decide how the job is built.

  1. You get in touchPhone, email or the form. We ask a couple of questions and book a visit.
  2. We visit and measureLevels, drainage, access and the materials that suit the house.
  3. You get a written proposalAn itemised quotation with the build-up, the programme and one fixed price. No pressure.
  4. We build itBuilt to the specification we quoted, kept tidy as we go, with your five year guarantee at handover.

Things people ask us

How much does a resin driveway cost in Rutland?
A resin bound driveway typically costs £150–£220 per m² all in, so a two-car drive of 40–50m² is usually £7,500–£11,000.
Which areas do you cover?
Rutland, including Oakham, Uppingham and the villages, plus Stamford, Market Harborough, Corby and Kettering.
Are you Vuba approved?
Yes. We install Vuba UV-stable resin systems to the manufacturer's specification.
Is there a guarantee?
Yes: a five year workmanship guarantee, in writing, on every job.
Do you charge for a site visit?
No. Site visits and written quotations are free.
