Bin stores in Greetham

Bin stores in Greetham

Anyone who has pulled a full wheelie bin across a gravel drive knows the problem. The wheels dig in, the bin tips, and the stones end up on the pavement. In Greetham, where many drives are only now being replaced after decades of gravel, it is a common reason people call us. We fit composite slatted stores with aluminium doors for one, two or three bins on a level paved pad, never on soil or gravel, with a firm route to the kerb.

If the drive itself is being widened or replaced, the bin store and its path can be built at the same time. If it is staying as gravel, a paved strip from the store to the road solves most of the trouble. We measure your bins, check the lids open fully inside the store and give a guide price on the visit, followed by a fixed price in writing, whether you are in Greetham itself or nearby in Stretton or Clipsham.

Greetham specifically

What makes Greetham different

Greetham sits on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which generally drains well, so most pads here need a good sub-base and a sensible fall rather than anything elaborate. Rutland's flood strategy does name Greetham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, so on low-lying plots we take more care over the base and where the run-off goes. In the historic core, stone-coloured paving for the pad suits the village better than grey.

On one of Greetham's stone cottages, a typical job is a one or two bin store at the side, on a stone-coloured pad. On the later infill and estate houses, it is usually a two or three bin store beside the drive, with a paved strip to the road replacing a stretch of old gravel.

Questions

Bin stores in Greetham: questions

What does a bin store cost in Greetham?
As a guide, a single wheelie bin store fitted on a level paved base starts from about £1,000. Two and three bin stores, and any new path to the kerb, are priced on the visit. If you want a paved strip across an existing gravel drive, that is part of the path pricing, and we confirm everything in writing.
Is planning permission needed for a bin store in Greetham?
Tucked at the side or back, a store is normally permitted development. Out front, a free-standing store technically falls outside householder permitted development, and we check that with Rutland County Council. Greetham has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, and stone-coloured materials suit the village.
Can I keep my gravel drive and still have an easy route for the bins?
Yes. A paved strip or path from the store to the kerb, edged so the gravel stays in place, gives the wheels a firm surface without replacing the whole drive. We plan its line so it is the shortest practical route on collection day.
Can the store sit on the gravel instead of a pad?
We do not build stores on gravel or soil. Gravel shifts, so the store goes out of level and the doors stop closing properly, and the bins still have to come out over loose stone. A level paved pad avoids both problems.
