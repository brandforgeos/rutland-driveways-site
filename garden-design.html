Garden design

Design before digging

Structural decisions, such as where the levels change, where the water goes and where the seating catches the evening sun, are cheap to change on paper and expensive to change in concrete. So they are settled before work starts, with materials chosen from samples against your own walls.

Garden design

Built in the right order

Groundwork, drainage and walls first; paving and steps next; then fencing, lighting, topsoil, turf and planting. That order stops finished surfaces being damaged by later trades.

Questions

Things people ask us

How much does garden design cost?
We design and build whole gardens in a set order: survey and levels first, then retaining walls, steps and drainage, then surfaces, boundaries, lighting and planting. A full rear garden typically costs £15,000–£35,000 and takes two to four weeks on site.
Can a garden be built in phases?
Yes. We plan the whole garden first, then build it in stages in an order that does not undo earlier work.
