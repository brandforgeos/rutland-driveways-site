Kettering specifically

What makes Kettering different

Ground around Kettering ranges from ironstone to clay, and new-build plots are often compacted subsoil under a thin skin of topsoil. Neither is a reason not to build, but both change the base design, so we check each garden first. Kettering has 26 conservation areas and a handful of site-specific Article 4 directions covering individual streets or buildings, so we check your address before planning anything visible from the road.

Behind a terrace off the Headlands, the typical kitchen is a narrow single run along one boundary, with a grill and a worktop and perhaps an under-counter fridge, built from materials carried through. On interwar and post-war estates, or in Barton Seagrave, gardens allow an L-shape with a sink. Hanwood Park plots often get a kitchen planned alongside a first proper patio.

Questions

Outdoor kitchens in Kettering: questions

How much is an outdoor kitchen in Kettering?
A Kettering outdoor kitchen with built-in cabinets, a worktop and appliance space starts from about £6,000. With a grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge fitted, it is typically £8,000–£20,000. Gas, power and water connections are priced on top, and terraced gardens often mean a long run from the house. Carrying materials through a terraced house can add time, which we build into the fixed written price.
Is planning needed for an outdoor kitchen in Kettering?
A kitchen in the back garden does not normally need planning permission; a roof or pergola over it must meet the outbuilding height and size limits. Kettering has 26 conservation areas and some older Article 4 directions covering individual streets or buildings, so we check your address with North Northamptonshire Council's records first.
We only have access through the house. Can you still build one?
Often, yes. Many terraced homes near the centre have no side access. We plan the materials and the build so they can be carried through, and we protect the floors along the route. It may affect the price, which is why we look before quoting.
Is the soil on a new-build plot good enough to build on?
Compacted subsoil can be firm in places and soft in others, and it often drains badly. We dig to see what is there and set the base depth and drainage around it rather than building on the topsoil.
