Permeable driveways in Cottesmore: what is involved

A permeable driveway lets rain soak through the surface and the base into the ground, instead of running onto the road. Resin bound, permeable block paving and stabilised gravel can all be built as permeable systems, but only on an open-graded base designed for the ground underneath.

Cottesmore is a large village north east of Oakham, with ironstone and limestone cottages in the old centre and a good deal of later housing, and Kendrew Barracks, the former RAF Cottesmore, just outside it. Family homes here often want a wider drive and a lower-maintenance garden.

Permeable driveways in Cottesmore: questions

How much does a permeable driveway cost in Cottesmore?
As a guide, a permeable resin bound driveway costs £150–£220 per m², permeable block paving £140–£210 per m² and stabilised gravel £60–£100 per m², all in. On heavy clay a lined system with a controlled outlet adds to that. Your fixed price comes in writing after the site visit.
Do I need planning permission for a permeable driveway in Cottesmore?
A new or replacement front driveway needs no planning permission if it is permeable, or if the water drains to a lawn or border on your own land. More than 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road needs planning permission. Listed buildings and the Cottesmore conservation area have tighter rules, so we check your address before anything is designed.
Does the ground in Cottesmore make a difference?
How fast the ground takes water changes from street to street in Cottesmore, so we run a percolation test before choosing between a soakaway, a permeable base or a lined system with a controlled outlet.
Is a permeable driveway the same as a soakaway?
Not quite. A permeable drive spreads the water over the whole area and lets it soak away through the base. On ground that will not take water, it can be lined and drained to a controlled outlet instead.
Do permeable drives clog up?
Slowly, if leaves and silt are left on them. A sweep and an occasional jet wash keep the joints and pores open.
