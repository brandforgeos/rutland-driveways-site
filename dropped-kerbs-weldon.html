A dropped kerb in Weldon needs approval from North Northamptonshire Council before any work touches the footway or verge: £333 for the licence and inspection, with the crossing built by an approved contractor, and we can build it for you. The parking space must be at least 5m deep and 3m wide, the crossing at least 10m from a junction and 1m from a tree, and A, B and C roads need planning permission first.

Getting a dropped kerb approved in Weldon

  1. Check the basicsThe parking space must be at least 5m deep and 3m wide, the crossing at least 10m from a junction and 1m from a tree, and A, B and C roads need planning permission first. A decision usually takes 28 days, and work starts 2–3 weeks after the licence is issued.
  2. Planning first if neededYou need planning permission before the kerb application if the house is on a classified (A, B or C) road or is listed.
  3. Apply to the highway authority£333 licence and inspection fee. North Northamptonshire Council: dropped kerbs
  4. Build the crossingBuilt by a contractor approved by the council, which we can do for you. We hold the NRSWA street works qualification and can build the crossing for you, then build the driveway behind it so the levels line up.
  5. Build the drivewayThe drive behind the kerb is built to drain to your own ground, so no water runs onto the road.

Ground, drainage and conservation in Weldon

Weldon sits over the limestone and ironstone that were quarried here, and ground can change from natural rock to clay or fill within a garden. The Willow Brook runs past the village, so drainage needs designing rather than assuming.

Weldon has a conservation area covering the historic village, appraised in 2009. North Northamptonshire has no Article 4 direction in Weldon affecting driveways, porches or extensions.

Dropped kerbs in Weldon: questions

How much does a dropped kerb cost in Weldon?
North Northamptonshire Council charges £333 licence and inspection fee. The crossing itself is priced after the site visit.
Who builds the dropped kerb in Weldon?
A contractor approved by the council, which we can do for you. We can build it under the permit.
How long does a dropped kerb take in Weldon?
A decision usually takes 28 days, and work starts 2–3 weeks after the licence is issued.
Do I need planning permission for a dropped kerb?
Only on a classified (A, B or C) road, for a listed building, or where an Article 4 direction applies. Otherwise the highway authority's consent is what you need.
