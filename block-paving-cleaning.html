Block paving cleaning

What the job involves

A rotary clean at a pressure that lifts dirt without blasting the sand out of the joints or scarring the blocks, then a weed and moss treatment, fresh kiln-dried jointing sand brushed in, and a sealer if you want one to lock the sand and resist stains.

Block paving cleaning

When cleaning is not enough

If blocks have sunk, rocked or spread, the base or the edges have failed and cleaning will not fix it. We will tell you on the visit and price a repair instead.

Questions

Things people ask us

How much does block paving cleaning cost?
Most tired block drives are dirty and empty of jointing sand rather than worn out. Washed at the right pressure, treated for weeds and moss, re-sanded with kiln-dried sand and sealed, they come back remarkably well, and it is worth doing every three to five years.
Will pressure washing damage my block paving?
Not at the right pressure with a rotary head. Blasting with a narrow lance is what strips the joints and marks the blocks.
