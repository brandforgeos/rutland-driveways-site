Questions

Things people ask us

How much does patio repairs cost?
Most patio problems, such as rocking slabs, cracks, failed pointing and puddles, come from slabs that were never fully bedded. Individual slabs can be lifted and re-bedded, pointing raked out and redone and falls corrected; where the whole patio is failing, we say so before you spend money on it.
Why do my patio slabs rock?
Usually because they were laid on spots of mortar rather than a full bed, so they flex and the mortar breaks up. The fix is to lift them and bed them properly.
