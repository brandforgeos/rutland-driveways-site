Driveway drainage in Empingham

Driveway drainage in Empingham

Empingham sits at the eastern end of Rutland Water, below the dam, and many of its homes have generous plots with long drives and open country beyond. More drive means more hard surface collecting rain, and on a long, gently falling drive water can travel a fair distance before it pools somewhere awkward, usually at a garage, a gate or the bottom of the slope. We look at the whole length before deciding where a channel should go and how much of it is needed.

The village centre around the church and the main street is stone-built, with natural materials the safe choice in the conservation area, and later houses sit on its edges. The North Brook, a tributary of the Gwash, runs through the village, and Rutland's flood strategy traces it on through Cottesmore, Empingham and Ryhall. Where your drive sits relative to that brook changes the drainage design more than anything else. We test the ground near it before relying on a soakaway.

Empingham specifically

What makes Empingham different

Empingham is on the limestone side of Rutland and much of the village drains reasonably well, so a soakaway sized from a percolation test is often the neat answer. Ground near the North Brook stays wet, though, and there a soakaway can struggle, so we may recommend a permeable area or a different outlet. Empingham has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, and drainage on your own driveway does not need planning permission. Water must not be sent onto the road or into the foul sewer.

A typical Empingham job is a detached house with a long gravel, tarmac or block drive where water gathers near the garage or the entrance. We place a heavy-duty channel where the flow collects, sometimes with a second short run, take it to a soakaway sized from a percolation test, and correct falls along the length where the base has dipped.

Driveway drainage in Empingham: questions

What will drainage cost on a long Empingham drive?
A channel across a typical driveway or garage entrance is about £1,000–£1,800 installed. Where longer runs are needed, a heavy-duty, vehicle-rated channel is £120–£200 per metre, and soakaways are sized and priced after a percolation test. These are guide prices; the fixed price comes in writing after we visit.
Do I need planning permission for drainage in Empingham?
Drainage work on your own Empingham driveway does not need planning permission, and the village has no Article 4 direction. In the conservation area natural materials are the safe choice for anything visible. As everywhere, drive water must be kept out of the foul sewer and off the road.
Do we need a channel along the whole length of our drive?
Rarely. On most long drives water collects at one or two predictable points, and a channel across each, with corrected falls, deals with it. We walk the drive, look at where water sits after rain, and only specify what is needed.
We live near the North Brook. What changes?
Ground near the brook stays wet, so a soakaway that would work elsewhere in the village may fill and stay full. We run a percolation test before designing, and if the ground will not take the water we look at a permeable area or another safe route.
