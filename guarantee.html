Guarantee

What it covers

Every driveway, patio, garden and building job comes with a five year workmanship guarantee in writing. If our work fails within five years, we come back and put it right.

Resin systems also carry the manufacturer's product backing when installed to Vuba's specification, which is why we install them exactly to that specification.

  • Covers our workmanship: bases, laying, jointing, edging, drainage we built
  • Does not cover damage, misuse, or ground movement outside our work
  • Ring the number you already have and we come and look
