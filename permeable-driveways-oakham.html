Permeable driveways in Oakham

Permeable driveways in Oakham

Oakham is listed in Rutland County Council's flood strategy as a surface water flood risk area of national significance, which makes the way a driveway handles rain more than a technicality here. A permeable driveway lets rain soak through the surface and the base, instead of running off onto the road and into drains that are already under pressure. Rutland Driveways & Landscaping builds them in resin bound, permeable block paving and stabilised gravel, each on an open-graded base designed for the ground underneath.

The catch in Oakham is that much of the town sits on heavy clay that holds water through the winter. A permeable surface on clay still needs somewhere for the water to go, so on many plots we line the base and drain it to a controlled outlet rather than relying on the ground to soak it up. We decide which approach suits your drive after digging a test hole on the visit, and the fixed price follows in writing.

Oakham specifically

What makes Oakham different

Three kinds of street shape the work here. In the old town around the castle, All Saints and the Market Place, the conservation area covers the historic centre, though Oakham has no Article 4 direction, so permitted development applies outside listed buildings with the usual conservation area limits. Off Brooke Road, Braunston Road and Burley Road sit Victorian and interwar terraces and semis. At Barleythorpe and Oakham Heights, builder-grade drives often come up within a few years, and the slow-draining Vale ground beneath them is the same.

A typical Oakham job is replacing a tired builder-grade drive on one of the newer estates, or the frontage of an interwar semi where old concrete sheds water onto the pavement. We break out the old surface, check the clay with a test hole, then build an open-graded sub-base, lined with a controlled outlet where the ground will not take the water, before laying the finish.

Questions

Permeable driveways in Oakham: questions

How much does a permeable driveway cost in Oakham?
Guide prices are £150–£220 per m² for permeable resin bound, £140–£210 per m² for permeable block paving and £60–£100 per m² for stabilised gravel, all in. Because so much of Oakham is heavy clay, a lined system with a controlled outlet often adds to that. You get a fixed price in writing after the visit.
Do I need planning permission for a new drive in Oakham?
Not if the new or replacement front driveway is permeable, or if its water drains to a lawn or border on your own land. Oakham has no Article 4 direction, so that applies outside listed buildings, with the usual conservation area limits in the historic centre. Rutland County Council is the planning authority.
Why does Oakham's clay change the design?
Vale of Catmose clay holds water through the winter, so rain that passes through a permeable surface can sit in the base instead of soaking away. On those plots we line the base and drain it to a controlled outlet, which decides where the water goes instead of leaving it trapped or letting it run onto the road.
My Barleythorpe drive is only a few years old. Is it worth replacing?
Builder-grade drives on the newer Oakham estates are often replaced within a few years. If yours is sinking, cracking or holding puddles, we look at the base as well as the surface, because base depth and drainage are where Oakham drives succeed or fail. Rebuilding it as a permeable system also settles where the rain goes.
