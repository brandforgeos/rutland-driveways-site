Weldon specifically

Homes, ground and conservation in Weldon

Weldon is an old stone village on the east side of Corby, famous for Weldon stone, the limestone quarried here for centuries. Its historic centre of stone houses is surrounded by later estates that now join it to Corby.

Weldon sits over the limestone and ironstone that were quarried here, and ground can change from natural rock to clay or fill within a garden. The Willow Brook runs past the village, so drainage needs designing rather than assuming.

Weldon has a conservation area covering the historic village, appraised in 2009. North Northamptonshire has no Article 4 direction in Weldon affecting driveways, porches or extensions.

The Willow Brook runs past Weldon, where the Environment Agency operates the Weldon flood storage reservoir. Check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Sources: Weldon conservation area (Northamptonshire HER) · North Northamptonshire conservation areas.

North Northamptonshire Council

Council rules and fees for Weldon

  • Dropped kerbs: £333 licence and inspection fee; built by a contractor approved by the council, which we can do for you. North Northamptonshire Council: dropped kerbs
  • Front driveways: A permeable drive, or one that drains to your own lawn or border, needs no planning permission; more than 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road does. Planning Portal guidance
  • Planning: householder applications cost £548 (national fee from April 2026); pre-application advice from North Northamptonshire Council costs £187. Pre-application advice
  • Building regulations: North Northamptonshire Council building control. Garage conversion quoted by the council for each job (no published schedule); extension quoted by the council for each job. Building control
  • Trees: six weeks' notice for trees in a conservation area, consent for protected trees; both free. North Northamptonshire: tree protection

Checked October 2026.
