Permeable driveways in Ketton

Permeable driveways in Ketton

Ketton gave its name to Ketton stone, the cream oolitic limestone quarried beside the village, and its older streets are built of it, many under Collyweston slate. A grey concrete drive sits uneasily against that, which is why buff and natural tones suit Ketton frontages better. Permeable resin bound, permeable block paving and stabilised gravel can all be chosen in warmer colours, and each lets rain soak through the surface and an open-graded base into the ground rather than onto the road.

Below the village is Lincolnshire Limestone, which usually drains well and suits a permeable build-up. The exception is near the River Chater, which runs through the village to meet the Welland. The Welland flood warning area at Ketton is the only Environment Agency flood warning area in Rutland, so plots near the water need testing before we design. Rutland Driveways & Landscaping gives a fixed written price once that check is done.

Ketton specifically

What makes Ketton different

Ketton has a conservation area covering the historic village but no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings, with the usual conservation area limits. Around the historic core are later 20th-century houses and bungalows, and the cement works and quarry sit on the edge of the village. Rutland's flood strategy names Ketton among the places with a few properties at river flood risk, and those are the homes where we look hardest at where drive water ends up.

A typical Ketton job is a later 20th-century bungalow on the edge of the historic core, where an old tarmac or concrete drive is replaced with a buff resin bound or stabilised gravel finish that sits better with the stone around it. On the limestone the base can usually let water soak away; near the Chater we test first and may line it.

Questions

Permeable driveways in Ketton: questions

How much does a permeable drive cost in Ketton?
Guide prices run from £60–£100 per m² for stabilised gravel to £140–£210 per m² for permeable block paving and £150–£220 per m² for permeable resin bound, all in. Most Ketton plots on the limestone can soak away through the base; if a plot by the Chater needs lining, that adds to the cost. The fixed price follows in writing.
Do I need planning permission for a permeable drive in Ketton?
Ketton has no Article 4 direction, so outside listed buildings normal permitted development applies, with the usual conservation area limits in the historic village. Under those rules, a permeable new or replacement front drive needs no planning permission, and neither does one draining to a lawn or border on your own land.
What colour of drive suits a Ketton stone house?
Ketton stone frontages call for buff and natural tones rather than grey. In practice that points to a warm-toned resin bound, buff permeable block or a natural stabilised gravel. We talk through colours on the visit, looking at them against your own stonework.
We live near the Chater. Will the ground take the water?
It may not. The limestone that drains well across most of Ketton behaves differently close to the River Chater, and the village sits within the Welland flood warning area. We dig a test hole first; if the ground will not take water, a lined system with a controlled outlet is the alternative.
