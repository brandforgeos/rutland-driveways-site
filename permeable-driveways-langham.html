Permeable driveways in Langham

Permeable driveways in Langham

Langham is one of the Rutland villages with an Article 4 direction, and that changes the usual picture for front driveways. Rutland's Article 4 directions can cover front walls, gates and fences, parking spaces, porches and roofs, so before designing anything facing the road we check what the Langham direction covers. Elsewhere, a permeable surface is often the simple way to avoid a planning application; here, it may not be the whole answer.

The second factor is the ground. Langham sits on the edge of the Vale of Catmose clay, where water drains slowly, so a permeable drive often needs its base lined and drained to a controlled outlet rather than left to soak away. We build in resin bound, permeable block paving and stabilised gravel, each on an open-graded base, and the fixed written price follows the visit and the planning check.

Langham specifically

What makes Langham different

Plots in Langham are often generous enough for sweeping drives, which means larger areas of surface and more rain to deal with. The village, long known as the home of Ruddles brewery, has ironstone and limestone houses in its centre and later housing around it, with a conservation area covering the historic part. Rutland's flood strategy notes that the Langham Brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity, and names Langham among the places with a few properties at river flood risk.

A typical Langham job is a sweeping drive on a generous plot, replacing worn tarmac or gravel with permeable block paving or resin bound. We check the Article 4 position first, then dig to see how the clay behaves. On clay that drains slowly, the open-graded base is lined and taken to a controlled outlet.

Permeable driveways in Langham: questions

How much does a permeable driveway cost in Langham?
Our guide prices are £140–£210 per m² for permeable block paving, £150–£220 per m² for permeable resin bound and £60–£100 per m² for stabilised gravel, all in. On Langham's heavy clay, a lined system with a controlled outlet adds to that, and sweeping drives cover a lot of ground, so the fixed written price follows the visit.
Does Langham's Article 4 direction affect my driveway?
It can. Rutland's Article 4 directions can cover front walls, gates and fences, parking spaces, porches and roofs, so we check the Langham direction against your property before designing anything facing the road. Where it does not bite, a permeable front drive, or one draining to a lawn or border on your own land, needs no planning permission.
Will a permeable drive soak away on Langham clay?
Often not on its own. The Vale clay at the edge of Langham drains slowly, so water passing through a permeable surface can sit in the base. We dig to see how your ground behaves, and where it will not take the water, we line the base and fit a controlled outlet.
We are near the Langham Brook. What should we think about?
The brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity, so for nearby homes it makes sense not to add fast runoff from a drive. A lined permeable base with a controlled outlet holds water back and releases it gradually, which is the approach we lean towards on clay near the brook.
