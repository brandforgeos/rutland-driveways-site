Louvred pergolas in Edith Weston

Louvred pergolas in Edith Weston

On the south shore of Rutland Water, Edith Weston's terraces, outdoor rooms and lighting are usually designed around the view, and a louvred pergola is often the centrepiece. The frame turns a terrace into a room: louvres open for sun, closed against rain, with warm-white LED lighting built in so the space works into the evening. Because the frame is freestanding, it can be placed where the outlook is best rather than wherever the house wall happens to be, and sized to the terrace rather than the other way round.

Sides make the difference to how it feels. Glass walls with sliding doors keep the outlook while cutting out a breeze; privacy louvres or blinds suit the sides facing neighbours. Frames run up to 8m × 3m, which suits the substantial houses here, and in black, anthracite, latte or light stone. Edith Weston has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction, and natural tones are the right choice in it.

Edith Weston specifically

What makes Edith Weston different

Edith Weston is on the limestone plateau above the reservoir, and most plots drain reasonably well, so the water that runs down the posts can often go to a soakaway. Clay pockets do turn up, and they change both drainage and the base, so we test the ground before designing pads or a terrace. Rutland's flood strategy does not name Edith Weston for river flooding. In the conservation area a pergola cannot sit between the side of the house and the boundary.

A typical Edith Weston job is a large pergola, 6m × 3m or more, over a stone terrace behind a substantial house, with glass walls on the view side, louvres or blinds facing neighbours, motorised louvres and LED lighting. It often goes in alongside a new or extended terrace, built so the base under each post is sound.

PlanningA freestanding pergola in a rear garden is normally permitted development if it stays behind the front of the house, is no more than 2.5m high at the eaves and 2.5m overall within 2m of a boundary, does not sit on a platform more than 30cm high, and together with any sheds or other outbuildings covers no more than half the garden. A listed house needs planning permission for one. In a conservation area it also cannot go between the side of the house and the boundary. Planning Portal: outbuildings

Louvred pergolas in Edith Weston: questions

What does a louvred pergola cost in Edith Weston?
From £4,500 supplied and fitted for a 3m × 4m frame on a suitable existing base. Larger frames, glass walls with sliding doors, motorised louvres and a new or extended terrace are priced after the site visit, followed by a fixed price in writing.
Is planning permission needed for a pergola in Edith Weston?
Edith Weston has a conservation area and no Article 4 direction. A freestanding rear-garden pergola within permitted development limits normally needs no application, but in the conservation area it cannot sit beside the house. For a listed house, Rutland County Council's planning permission is required first.
Can the lighting be used for evening entertaining?
Warm-white LED lighting is built into the frame, so the pergola lights itself without separate fittings. With the louvres closed and glass walls or blinds on the sides, it works as an outdoor room after dark.
Can a pergola go on a raised terrace facing the water?
It can, but a platform more than 30cm high takes the pergola outside permitted development. On a raised terrace we check heights first, and if needed we discuss a planning application before going further.
