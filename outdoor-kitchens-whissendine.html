Outdoor kitchens in Whissendine

Outdoor kitchens in Whissendine

Bigger gardens and longer drives are common along Whissendine's main street, and that shapes an outdoor kitchen project. The further the kitchen sits from the house, the longer the gas, power and water runs, and those connections are priced on top. We show you the trade-off on the visit: a kitchen near the back door is cheaper to service, while one at the far end may catch the evening sun.

Whichever you choose, the build is the same standard: a concrete base, block or stone-faced counters, weatherproof worktops and space for the grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge. Our prices are guides until the fixed written price follows the visit, when we have measured the runs and looked at how wet the ground is. We cover Whissendine, Langham, Teigh, Ashwell, Pickwell and Stapleford, and travel to Oakham and Cottesmore too.

Whissendine specifically

What makes Whissendine different

Whissendine is on Vale clay and the Whissendine Brook runs through the village. Rutland's flood strategy records that Main Street floods and becomes impassable, and names the village among those with a few properties at river flood risk. So we design the base and the water route before anything else. Whissendine is not a conservation area and has no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development rules apply outside listed buildings.

Behind a stone or brick house or a farm conversion on the main street, a typical kitchen is a generous L or U-shape with a grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge, sited on higher ground within a long garden. Newer homes in the village tend to keep the kitchen closer to the house, beside a patio.

Questions

Outdoor kitchens in Whissendine: questions

What does an outdoor kitchen in Whissendine cost?
A built-in Whissendine kitchen with cabinets, worktop and appliance space starts from about £6,000; fully equipped with grill, pizza oven, sink and fridge, it is typically £8,000–£20,000. Gas, power and water connections are priced on top, so a long run down the garden adds to the cost.
Does Whissendine have any planning restrictions for outdoor kitchens?
Whissendine is not a conservation area and has no Article 4 direction. In a rear garden, an outdoor kitchen does not normally need planning permission, though a roof or pergola over it has to meet the outbuilding height and size limits. Rutland County Council is the council to ask.
Should we avoid putting the kitchen near the brook?
Where there is a choice, yes. Ground near the Whissendine Brook is wetter, and the village has known flooding. We look for the higher, drier part of the garden and keep the electrics and fridge there.
Can we start without gas and add it later?
Yes. Bottled gas, charcoal and wood all suit plenty of grills and pizza ovens. If you think you will want a fixed supply later, we can plan a duct route now so it is easier for a Gas Safe engineer to add.
