Driveway gates in Weldon

Driveway gates in Weldon

Weldon is famous for Weldon stone, the limestone quarried here for centuries, and ironstone was worked here too. That history is still in the ground. Within one garden, a gate post can find natural rock, clay or old fill, and each behaves differently under a heavy, automated gate. We dig where the posts will go before we design the footings, so both sides of the gate stay level. It is a small step that prevents a gate dropping out of line a year after it goes in.

The historic centre of stone houses is surrounded by later estates that now join Weldon to Corby. Older stone frontages often have walls and piers we can work with, while estate homes usually need new posts. We fit sliding and swing gates in aluminium and composite, automate them with keypads, intercoms or remote fobs, and force-test every automated gate to the standard after installation. Where the drive is short, a sliding gate keeps the parking space clear.

Weldon specifically

What makes Weldon different

Variable ground is the main issue in Weldon: natural limestone and ironstone give good bearing, but clay and fill from old workings give less, and the change can happen within a few metres. The Willow Brook runs past the village, where the Environment Agency operates the Weldon flood storage reservoir, so properties near it get electrics placed above low spots. Weldon's conservation area covers the historic village, appraised in 2009, but there is no Article 4 direction in Weldon affecting gates, so normal permitted development applies outside listed buildings.

A typical Weldon job is a house on one of the estates linking the village to Corby, where the owners want a sliding gate across a drive with a clear run of fence beside it. It usually involves a test dig, a concrete foundation along the gate's run, a cable from the house, the gate and motor, a keypad or fobs, and photocells and safety edges.

Questions

Driveway gates in Weldon: questions

How much are driveway gates in Weldon?
In Weldon, a new automated sliding gate typically costs £5,000–£10,000 supplied and fitted, and automated swing gates £4,000–£8,000. Gates you already have can usually be automated for £1,500–£3,000. The power supply is priced on top. Because the ground varies so much, we dig before pricing the footings and then send a fixed price in writing.
Is planning permission needed for gates in Weldon?
Seldom. North Northamptonshire has no Article 4 direction in Weldon affecting gates, so they are normally permitted development up to 2m high, or 1m next to a road used by vehicles, as long as they do not open outwards over the footway or road. In the conservation area or on a listed house, road-facing gates can need permission.
Can my old stone gate piers take an automated gate?
Sometimes. Old piers were built for hand-opened gates, and a motor adds load and vibration. We check whether they are sound and well founded. If they are not, we can set new posts on proper footings behind or beside them.
Why does Weldon ground need checking before a gate goes in?
Because centuries of quarrying left it variable. A post footing on natural stone behaves very differently from one in clay or fill. We dig at each post position and design the foundation for what we find, so the gate stays square and the motor is not strained.
