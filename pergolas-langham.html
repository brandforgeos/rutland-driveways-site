Louvred pergolas in Langham

Louvred pergolas in Langham

Langham is one of the Rutland villages with an Article 4 direction on its conservation area. Rutland's directions can cover front walls, gates and fences, parking spaces, porches and roofs, so we read the Langham direction before designing anything that faces the road. A freestanding pergola in a rear garden is normally permitted development, but in the conservation area it cannot sit between the side of the house and the boundary, and listed houses need planning permission.

With that settled, Langham's plots give a pergola room. Gardens are often generous, which means a frame can be anywhere from 3m × 3m to 8m × 3m and placed to catch the evening sun rather than squeezed against the house. The aluminium frame stands on its own posts, the louvres open and close to suit the weather, and LED lighting is built in. On a wide plot, a mix of open sides and privacy louvres lets you shelter the seating from one direction while keeping the garden in view.

Langham specifically

What makes Langham different

Langham sits on the edge of the Vale of Catmose clay, where water drains slowly, and the Langham Brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity. Both matter for a pergola. The rain the roof collects comes down the posts, and on clay a soakaway needs testing before we rely on it. The base matters too: a patio laid thinly over clay can move, so we often set the posts on concrete pads. Plots near the brook get the most care.

A typical Langham job is a larger pergola, often 6m × 3m or more, over a broad rear terrace behind an ironstone or limestone house, or standing on new pads in a full rear garden, with LED lighting and one or two sides closed by louvres or blinds, and the post water taken to a tested soakaway or channel.

Questions

Louvred pergolas in Langham: questions

How much does a pergola cost in Langham?
A Langham pergola begins at £4,500 supplied and fitted, as a guide for a 3m × 4m louvred frame on a suitable existing base. Larger frames suit many Langham gardens; those, along with concrete pads, drainage, motorised louvres and side screens, are priced after the visit, then confirmed in writing as a fixed price.
Does Langham's Article 4 direction affect a pergola?
Rutland's Article 4 directions can cover front walls, gates and fences, parking spaces, porches and roofs, so we check the Langham direction for your address. A rear-garden pergola within permitted development limits normally needs no application, but it cannot go beside the house in the conservation area, and listed houses need permission.
My garden is near the Langham Brook. What changes?
Mainly the drainage. The brook has a history of exceeding its bank capacity and the clay around it drains slowly, so a soakaway may sit full. We test the ground and may route the post water to a channel or existing drain instead.
How big can a pergola be on a large Langham plot?
Frames run up to 8m × 3m. On a big plot, the limits are usually permitted development ones: behind the front of the house, 2.5m at the eaves, 2.5m overall within 2m of a boundary, and with outbuildings covering no more than half the garden.
