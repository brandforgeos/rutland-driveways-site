Driveway drainage in Oakham

Driveway drainage in Oakham

Rutland County Council's flood strategy lists Oakham as a surface water flood risk area of national significance, and that matches what we see on driveways across the town. Water that has nowhere to go is the commonest problem we fix here: puddles that sit for days, a sheet of water across the garage mouth, or rain running off the drive towards the front door. Rutland Driveways & Landscaping sorts out the cause, not just the symptom, with channel drains, soakaways sized for the ground and falls put right.

Oakham's drives come in three broad types, and each fails in its own way. In the old town around the castle, All Saints and the Market Place, frontages are small and often hemmed in by limestone and ironstone walls. The Victorian and interwar semis off Brooke Road, Braunston Road and Burley Road tend to have drives that were laid, relaid and patched over decades. On the newer estates at Barleythorpe and Oakham Heights, builder-grade drives are often replaced within a few years, and poor drainage is frequently part of the reason.

Oakham specifically

What makes Oakham different

Most of the ground around Oakham is heavy clay that stays wet all winter, so a soakaway that would empty overnight on limestone can sit full here for weeks. That is why we dig a test hole on the visit and size any soakaway or crate system from a percolation test, and why we sometimes recommend a permeable area or a different route for the water instead. The Barleythorpe Brook runs culverted under parts of the town. In the conservation area there is no Article 4 direction, and drainage on your own drive does not need planning permission.

A typical Oakham job is a semi or terrace with a concrete or block drive that falls towards the garage or the house. We cut in a heavy-duty channel across the low point, bed and haunch it in concrete, and take the water to a soakaway or crate system sized for the clay, correcting the falls where the base has dropped so water stops collecting by the door.

Driveway drainage in Oakham: questions

How much does driveway drainage cost in Oakham?
Expect about £1,000–£1,800 installed for a channel drain across a typical Oakham driveway or garage entrance, or £120–£200 per metre for a heavy-duty, vehicle-rated channel. On Oakham clay the soakaway is the variable, so it is sized and priced after a percolation test. Treat these as guide prices; the fixed price comes in writing after the visit.
Do I need planning permission for drainage in Oakham?
Drainage on your own Oakham driveway does not need planning permission, and although the historic centre is a conservation area, the town has no Article 4 direction. The rules that do apply: surface water must not go into the foul sewer or onto the road, and any new connection to a public sewer needs the water company's approval.
Why is standing water such a common problem on Oakham drives?
The town sits on Vale of Catmose clay that holds water through the winter, and Oakham is named in the county flood strategy as a surface water risk area. Rain that lands on a hard drive has little chance of soaking away unless somewhere has been designed to take it.
Our Barleythorpe drive is only a few years old and already puddles. Why?
Builder-grade drives on the newer estates are often laid with shallow falls or a thin base that settles in places, and on clay the water then has nowhere to go. We check the falls, the base and the outlet before suggesting a channel, and fix the drive without replacing the whole surface where possible.
