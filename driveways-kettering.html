Driveways in Kettering

Driveways for every kind of Kettering house

From the tree-lined avenues of the Headlands to family homes in Barton Seagrave and Ise Lodge, Kettering's houses span a century and a half of good building — and their drives and gardens deserve the same standard. We install resin, block paving and tarmac driveways, porcelain patios and complete gardens across the town and its villages.

Kettering streets we work

The boot-and-shoe years left Kettering its best streets: the Headlands conservation area and the avenues around it, full of Victorian and Edwardian villas with brick detailing, original walls and frontages worth converting carefully. Tumbled block with contrasting borders and warm resin blends both belong here, laid out so gate piers and boundary walls keep doing their job.

Barton Seagrave and Ise Lodge bring the established family-home brief — double-width drives, tidy front gardens, generous rear gardens ready for porcelain terraces and proper lawns — while the growing developments around the town add new-build upgrade work. The villages — Geddington with its Eleanor Cross, Broughton, Pytchley, Cranford — give us the stone settings we know from home.

  • Conservation-aware finishes for the Headlands and period streets
  • Permeable resin and block as standard — drainage rules satisfied by design

Villages we serve around Kettering

We work across Kettering, the Headlands conservation area, Barton Seagrave and Ise Lodge, plus surrounding villages including Geddington, Broughton, Pytchley, Cranford and Weekley. About 30 minutes from our base.

Kettering specifically

Ground, drainage and conservation in Kettering

Ground around Kettering ranges from ironstone to clay, and new-build plots are often compacted subsoil under a thin layer of topsoil. We check what is under each garden before choosing the base and the drainage.

Kettering has 26 conservation areas, including the town centre, Barton Seagrave, Burton Latimer, Geddington and Rothwell. Its Article 4 directions are site-specific, mostly older ones covering individual streets or buildings, so we check your address before designing anything that faces the road.

The River Ise runs along the east of the town, with river level stations at Barford Bridge and Slade Brook.

Driveways in Kettering: questions

Are you local enough to Kettering?
Half an hour door to door — the same radius national installers cover from much further away, with the difference that you can visit our finished work between here and home.
Do you work in the Headlands conservation area?
Yes, and carefully: sympathetic materials and permeable construction keep most driveway projects outside the need for any application. We confirm the position at the site visit.
What about gardens?
Patios, lawns, fencing and full transformations — around half of what we do in Northamptonshire is behind the house, not in front of it.
How much does a new driveway cost in Kettering?
For a typical Kettering drive of 30–50m², resin bound costs roughly £4,500–£11,000, block paving £3,500–£8,500 and tarmac £2,750–£6,500, all in.
How do I get a dropped kerb in Kettering?
You apply to North Northamptonshire Council: £333 licence and inspection fee. It is built by a contractor approved by the council, which we can do for you. See dropped kerbs in Kettering.
Do I need planning permission for a driveway in Kettering?
A permeable drive, or one that drains to your own lawn or border, needs no planning permission; more than 5m² of impermeable surface draining to the road does.
