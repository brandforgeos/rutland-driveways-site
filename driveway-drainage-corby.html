Driveway drainage in Corby: what is involved

We fix puddling and flooding drives with channel drains across the slope, soakaways and crate systems sized for the ground, and corrected falls, so water leaves the drive without running to the road, the garage or the house.

Corby grew as a steel town: a New Town designated in 1950, with estates such as Kingswood, Lodge Park, Beanfield and Danesholme built for steelworkers, around the older villages of Corby Village, Great Oakley and Weldon. Priors Hall Park in the north east is planned for more than 5,000 homes, and many of its gardens are handed over as bare, compacted plots.

We cover Corby (NN17 and NN18) and the villages around it, including Weldon, Great Oakley, Corby Village, Gretton, Cottingham, Middleton, Rockingham, Stanion and East Carlton.

Ground and conservation in Corby

Corby sits on the Northampton Sand ironstone that fed the steelworks, and large areas around the town were opencast quarried and restored. Ground can change from natural ironstone to clay or fill within a garden, so we dig before we design the base and drainage. How fast the ground takes water changes from street to street in Corby, so we run a percolation test before choosing between a soakaway, a permeable base or a lined system with a controlled outlet.

Corby has nine conservation areas, including the Old Village, Cottingham and Middleton, Gretton, Rockingham, Stanion, Great Oakley and Weldon. Its only Article 4 direction (Kingswood) controls houses in multiple occupation and does not affect driveways, porches or extensions.

The Willow Brook runs through Corby and Weldon, with an Environment Agency flood storage reservoir at Weldon, so properties near the brook need drainage planned with care. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Driveway drainage in Corby: questions

How much does a driveway drainage cost in Corby?
A channel drain across a typical driveway or garage entrance costs about £1,000–£1,800 installed, or £120–£200 per metre for a heavy-duty, vehicle-rated channel. Soakaways are sized and priced after a percolation test. Your fixed price comes in writing after the site visit.
Do I need planning permission for a driveway drainage in Corby?
Drainage work on your own driveway does not need planning permission, but surface water must not go into the foul sewer or onto the road, and any new connection to a public sewer needs the water company's approval. Listed buildings and the Corby conservation area have tighter rules, so we check your address before anything is designed.
Does the ground in Corby make a difference?
How fast the ground takes water changes from street to street in Corby, so we run a percolation test before choosing between a soakaway, a permeable base or a lined system with a controlled outlet.
Why does my drive puddle?
Usually the falls are wrong, the base has sunk in places, or the water has nowhere to go at the bottom. We check which before suggesting a channel drain.
Can drive water go into the drains?
Not into the foul sewer. It has to go to a soakaway, a permeable area or, with approval, a surface water sewer.
