Driveway gates in Ketton

Driveway gates in Ketton

Ketton gave its name to Ketton stone, and the village's older streets are built of that cream oolitic limestone, many under Collyweston slate. A gate in front of a house like that should sit quietly with the stone, which usually means buff and natural tones rather than grey. We fit swing and sliding gates in aluminium and composite, and we will talk through colours and styles that suit the frontage before anything is ordered.

Around the historic core, later 20th-century houses and bungalows have more conventional drives, often with room for a pair of swing gates opening inwards. We automate gates with keypads, intercoms or remote fobs, fit safety edges or photocells, and force-test every automated gate to the standard after installation. If you have sound gates already, automating them is usually £1,500–£3,000 plus the power supply. On the bungalows, a single sliding gate is often neater where the drive is short.

Ketton specifically

What makes Ketton different

Ketton sits on the Lincolnshire Limestone, which usually gives firm, well-drained ground for gate posts. The exception is near the River Chater, which runs through the village to meet the Welland: Ketton has the only Environment Agency flood warning area in Rutland, and ground near the river behaves differently. There we keep control boxes high and avoid low-lying motor positions. The historic village is a conservation area, though Ketton has no Article 4 direction, so road-facing gates can need permission there but normal permitted development applies elsewhere.

A typical Ketton job is a later 20th-century bungalow or house on the edge of the historic core, where the owners want automated swing gates to keep dogs in and cars secure. It usually involves posts on concrete footings in the limestone, gates in a buff or natural finish to sit with nearby stone, a cable run from the house, and photocells across the opening.

Questions

Driveway gates in Ketton: questions

How much do gates cost in Ketton?
Ketton guide prices: automated swing gates typically £4,000–£8,000 supplied and fitted, a new automated sliding gate £5,000–£10,000, or usually £1,500–£3,000 to automate gates you already have. Power supply is charged separately. Your choice of finish to suit Ketton stone is included in the fixed price we put in writing after the visit.
Can I put gates up in Ketton conservation area without permission?
Ketton has no Article 4 direction, so normal permitted development applies outside listed buildings: gates up to 2m high, or 1m next to a road used by vehicles, not opening outwards over the footway or road. In the conservation area or on a listed house, gates facing the road can need permission from Rutland County Council.
What colour gate suits a Ketton stone house?
Ketton stone is a cream limestone, and buff and natural tones generally sit better against it than grey. Aluminium and composite gates come in a range of finishes, and we bring samples to the visit so you can see them against your own walls before choosing.
We are near the River Chater. Will flooding damage the motor?
We design to reduce that risk. Ketton has Rutland's only Environment Agency flood warning area, so near the river we keep the control box and connections as high as practical and avoid putting motors at the lowest point of the entrance.
