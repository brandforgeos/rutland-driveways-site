Resin driveways in Kettering

What resin driveways in Kettering usually involve

Resin bound is a smart choice for Kettering homes: a smooth, seamless, permeable driveway that drains freely, needs almost no upkeep and looks well against the town's red-brick Victorian frontages and its modern estates alike. As a Vuba approved installer we lay resin bound driveways across Kettering and the surrounding Northamptonshire villages, always on a properly built open-grade base so the surface lasts and stays SuDS-compliant. We also lay block paving, tarmac and bound gravel where they suit the property better.

Why resin bound works so well in Kettering

Kettering runs from red-brick Victorian terraces near the centre to large modern estates on the edges, and resin bound suits both.

It drains. Resin bound is laid over an open-grade permeable base, so rainwater soaks straight through instead of running off. That keeps you on the right side of the rules on paving front gardens, usually without separate soakaways.

It suits the frontage. Warmer aggregate blends complement Kettering's red brick and ironstone, while cooler greys work on the newer estates. We bring samples and judge them against your actual frontage in daylight.

It lasts and stays tidy. A seamless resin surface has no joints for weeds, does not scatter like loose gravel or crack like old concrete, and needs little more than an occasional wash.

Built properly, from the base up

A resin driveway is only as good as what sits beneath it. We excavate to the right depth, lay and compact a permeable sub-base and an open-grade binder course, then lay the resin and aggregate — so the finished surface stays sound and free-draining for years. Skipping the base is why cheap resin fails, and we never do.

Ground, drainage and conservation in Kettering

Ground around Kettering ranges from ironstone to clay, and new-build plots are often compacted subsoil under a thin layer of topsoil. We check what is under each garden before choosing the base and the drainage.

Kettering has 26 conservation areas, including the town centre, Barton Seagrave, Burton Latimer, Geddington and Rothwell. Its Article 4 directions are site-specific, mostly older ones covering individual streets or buildings, so we check your address before designing anything that faces the road.

The River Ise runs along the east of the town, with river level stations at Barford Bridge and Slade Brook. You can check any address on the Environment Agency flood map.

Resin driveways in Kettering: questions

Is resin bound tough enough for daily use?
Yes. Resin bound laid over a compacted base handles daily traffic, deliveries and turning without rutting, and because it is one seamless surface there are no joints for weeds or moss to get into — a real advantage on the shadier plots common behind Kettering's terraces.
How long does a resin driveway take?
Once the base is ready, laying the resin itself is quick — usually a single dry day for an average drive. The groundwork before it takes longer, and we build in time for the base to be done properly rather than rushed.
What colours can I have?
A wide range of natural aggregate blends. We bring samples to your home and look at them against your house before you decide.
